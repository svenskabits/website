## Fillers and conversational transitions

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **eh** | um, uh | Jag tänkte, **eh**, att vi kunde börja nu. | I was thinking, um, that we could start now. |
| **alltså** | I mean, so | **Alltså**, jag förstår inte varför de ändrade beslutet. | I mean, I don't understand why they changed the decision. |
| **liksom** | like, sort of (informal) | Det blev **liksom** en paus mitt i samtalet. | There was sort of a pause in the middle of the conversation. |
| **typ** | about, roughly (informal) | Vi var **typ** tjugo personer på mötet. | There were about twenty of us at the meeting. |
| **så** | so, well | **Så**, var var vi innan vi blev avbrutna? | So, where were we before we were interrupted? |
| **förresten** | by the way | Har du **förresten** hört något från Erik? | By the way, have you heard anything from Erik? |

## Nuance and emphasis

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ju** | as you know, after all | Du har **ju** redan träffat henne. | You've already met her, after all. |
| **väl** | probably, I suppose | Det går **väl** att ordna till imorgon. | It should be possible to arrange by tomorrow. |
| **faktiskt** | actually, in fact | Jag tyckte **faktiskt** att föreläsningen var givande. | I actually thought the lecture was worthwhile. |
| **ändå** | still, anyway | Det var sent, men vi stannade **ändå** en stund. | It was late, but we stayed for a while anyway. |
| **nämligen** | you see, namely | Hon kunde inte följa med; hon var **nämligen** sjuk. | She couldn't come along; she was ill, you see. |
| **precis** | exactly, just | Det var **precis** vad jag tänkte säga. | That's exactly what I was about to say. |
| **i och för sig** | admittedly, in itself | **I och för sig** var förslaget rimligt, men det var dyrt. | Admittedly, the proposal was reasonable, but it was expensive. |
