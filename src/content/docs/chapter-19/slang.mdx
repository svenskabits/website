| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **schysst** | nice; fair (informal) | Det var **schysst** av dig att hjälpa mig. | It was nice of you to help me. |
| **najs** | nice; cool (informal) | Vi hittade ett **najs** café nära stationen. | We found a nice café near the station. |
| **grym** | great; amazing (informal) | Hon gjorde ett **grymt** jobb med presentationen. | She did an amazing job with the presentation. |
| **chilla** | to chill; relax (informal) | Vi **chillade** hemma efter jobbet. | We chilled at home after work. |
| **softa** | to take it easy (informal) | Jag tänker **softa** hemma i kväll. | I'm going to take it easy at home tonight. |
| **palla** | to have the energy or inclination (informal) | Jag **pallar** inte laga mat efter jobbet. | I can't be bothered to cook after work. |
| **orka** | to have the energy (colloquial) | Jag **orkar** inte diskutera det nu. | I don't have the energy to discuss it now. |
| **taggad** | excited; keen (informal) | Jag är **taggad** inför konserten. | I'm excited about the concert. |
| **nojig** | anxious; paranoid (informal) | Hon blev **nojig** när ingen svarade. | She got anxious when no one replied. |
| **lacka ur** | to lose one's temper (informal) | Han **lackade ur** när bussen var sen. | He lost his temper when the bus was late. |
| **fett** | really; very (slang intensifier) | Det var **fett** kul att träffa dem. | It was really fun to meet them. |
| **cringe** | embarrassing; awkward (slang) | Det blev **cringe** när han började sjunga. | It got awkward when he started singing. |
| **random** | unexpected; out of place (informal) | Det var en **random** kommentar mitt i mötet. | It was an unexpected comment in the middle of the meeting. |
| **en polare** | a buddy; a friend (informal) | Jag ska träffa en **polare** efter jobbet. | I'm meeting a friend after work. |
| **cash** | cash; money (informal) | Har du **cash** på dig? | Do you have any cash on you? |
