## Everyday clothes

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en tröja** | a sweater | Jag har en **tröja**. | I have a sweater. |
| **en t-shirt** | a T-shirt | Hon har en **t-shirt**. | She has a T-shirt. |
| **en skjorta** | a shirt | Han bär en **skjorta**. | He is wearing a shirt. |
| **en blus** | a blouse | Hon har en **blus**. | She has a blouse. |
| **ett par byxor** | a pair of trousers | Jag har **byxor** på mig. | I am wearing trousers. |
| **ett par jeans** | a pair of jeans | Jag har ett par **jeans**. | I have a pair of jeans. |
| **en kjol** | a skirt | Hon har en **kjol**. | She has a skirt. |
| **en klänning** | a dress | Hon har en **klänning**. | She has a dress. |
| **en jacka** | a jacket | Jag har en **jacka**. | I have a jacket. |
| **en kappa** | a coat | Han har en **kappa**. | He has a coat. |
