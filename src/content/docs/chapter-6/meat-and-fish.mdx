## Meat

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en köttbit** | a piece of meat | Jag steker en **köttbit**. | I am frying a piece of meat. |
| **en kyckling** | a chicken; chicken | Vi äter **kyckling** i dag. | We are eating chicken today. |
| **en korv** | a sausage | Han grillar en **korv**. | He is grilling a sausage. |
| **en köttbulle** | a meatball | Jag äter en **köttbulle**. | I am eating a meatball. |
| **en hamburgare** | a hamburger | Hon beställer en **hamburgare**. | She is ordering a hamburger. |
| **en biff** | a steak | Pappa lagar en **biff**. | Dad is cooking a steak. |
| **en skinka** | ham | Jag har **skinka** på smörgåsen. | I have ham on the sandwich. |

## Fish

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en fisk** | a fish; fish | Vi äter **fisk** på fredag. | We eat fish on Friday. |
| **en lax** | a salmon; salmon | Jag lagar **lax** till middag. | I am cooking salmon for dinner. |
| **en torsk** | a cod; cod | Han köper **torsk** i affären. | He is buying cod at the shop. |
| **en sill** | a herring; herring | De äter **sill** till lunch. | They eat herring for lunch. |
| **en räka** | a shrimp | Hon äter en **räka**. | She is eating a shrimp. |
| **en fiskfilé** | a fish fillet | Jag steker en **fiskfilé**. | I am frying a fish fillet. |
