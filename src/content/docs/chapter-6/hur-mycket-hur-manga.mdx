Use **hur mycket** and **hur många** to ask about quantity. Choose the phrase according to what you are asking about:

| Question phrase | Use it to ask about | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **hur mycket** | an amount, a price, or a degree | **Hur mycket vatten vill du ha?** — How much water would you like? |
| **hur många** | a number of countable things | **Hur många äpplen vill du ha?** — How many apples would you like? |

## Hur mycket: amounts and prices

Use **hur mycket** with things thought of as an amount rather than as separate items. The noun is usually singular and has no article after **hur mycket**:

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Hur mycket mjölk behöver vi?** | How much milk do we need? |
| **Hur mycket kaffe dricker du?** | How much coffee do you drink? |
| **Hur mycket tid har vi?** | How much time do we have? |

Use **hur mycket** to ask the price of something. The thing being priced is often named as the subject after the verb **kostar**:

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Hur mycket kostar den här kanelbullen?** | How much does this cinnamon bun cost? |
| **Hur mycket kostar biljetterna?** | How much do the tickets cost? |
| **Hur mycket kostar det?** | How much does it cost? |

You can also name a price unit and ask how many of those units something costs. In that case, use **hur många** with the countable unit:

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Hur många kronor kostar kaffet?** | How many kronor does the coffee cost? |
| **Hur många euro kostar biljetten?** | How many euros does the ticket cost? |

## Hur många: countable things

Use **hur många** before a plural noun when you can count the things one by one. The noun is normally in its indefinite plural form, with no article:

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Hur många bananer vill du ha?** | How many bananas would you like? |
| **Hur många personer kommer i kväll?** | How many people are coming tonight? |
| **Hur många barn har de?** | How many children do they have? |

The noun remains plural even when the answer is one. The answer itself can be a number or a quantity phrase:

| Question and answer | English |
|---|---|
| **Hur många äpplen vill du ha? – Ett, tack.** | How many apples would you like? – One, please. |
| **Hur många gäster kommer? – Tio personer.** | How many guests are coming? – Ten people. |

## Measuring an amount

When you ask for an amount measured in countable units, use **hur många** with the unit, even if what is being measured is normally an amount:

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Hur många liter mjölk ska vi köpa?** | How many litres of milk should we buy? |
| **Hur många kilo potatis behöver du?** | How many kilos of potatoes do you need? |
| **Hur många flaskor vatten har vi?** | How many bottles of water do we have? |

If you are asking about the amount itself, not its measurement units, use **hur mycket**: **Hur mycket mjölk ska vi köpa?** (How much milk should we buy?)

## Word order in questions

In a direct question, put **hur mycket** or **hur många** first, followed by the verb. If the question also has a subject, the subject usually comes after the verb:

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Hur många äpplen köper du?** | How many apples are you buying? |
| **Hur mycket kaffe dricker hon?** | How much coffee does she drink? |
| **Hur mycket kostar maten?** | How much does the food cost? |

When the question phrase itself is the subject, it comes before the verb:

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Hur många personer kommer?** | How many people are coming? |
| **Hur mycket vatten finns det kvar?** | How much water is left? |

## Asking about degree

**Hur mycket** can also ask about the degree of an action, feeling, or quality. In these questions it does not introduce a noun:

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Hur mycket tycker du om kaffe?** | How much do you like coffee? |
| **Hur mycket kostar det att bo här?** | How much does it cost to live here? |

Remember: **hur mycket** asks about an amount, price, or degree; **hur många** asks for the number of countable things or measurement units.
