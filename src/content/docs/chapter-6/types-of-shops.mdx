| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en mataffär** | a grocery store | **Mataffären** säljer mat. | The grocery store sells food. |
| **ett apotek** | a pharmacy | Jag köper medicin på **apoteket**. | I buy medicine at the pharmacy. |
| **en klädaffär** | a clothes shop | Hon köper en tröja i **klädaffären**. | She buys a sweater at the clothes shop. |
| **en skoaffär** | a shoe shop | Jag köper skor i **skoaffären**. | I buy shoes at the shoe shop. |
| **en bokhandel** | a bookshop | Jag köper en bok i **bokhandeln**. | I buy a book at the bookshop. |
| **en kiosk** | a kiosk | Jag köper en tidning i **kiosken**. | I buy a newspaper at the kiosk. |
| **en blomsterbutik** | a flower shop | Jag köper blommor i **blomsterbutiken**. | I buy flowers at the flower shop. |
| **en leksaksaffär** | a toy shop | Barnet får en leksak från **leksaksaffären**. | The child gets a toy from the toy shop. |
| **en möbelaffär** | a furniture store | Vi köper en soffa i **möbelaffären**. | We buy a sofa at the furniture store. |
| **en elektronikbutik** | an electronics store | Han köper en telefon i **elektronikbutiken**. | He buys a phone at the electronics store. |
| **en järnaffär** | a hardware store | Jag köper verktyg i **järnaffären**. | I buy tools at the hardware store. |
| **ett varuhus** | a department store | **Varuhuset** säljer många saker. | The department store sells many things. |
