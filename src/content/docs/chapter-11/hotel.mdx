## Booking and staying

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ett hotell** | a hotel | Vi bodde på ett **hotell** nära stationen. | We stayed at a hotel near the station. |
| **en bokning** | a booking | Jag har en **bokning** för två personer. | I have a booking for two people. |
| **boka** | to book | Hon **bokade** ett rum på nätet. | She booked a room online. |
| **avboka** | to cancel a booking | Vi måste **avboka** rummet. | We have to cancel the room. |
| **en gäst** | a guest | **Gästen** väntar i receptionen. | The guest is waiting at reception. |
| **bo** | to stay, to live | De **bor** på hotell i natt. | They are staying at a hotel tonight. |
| **checka in** | to check in | Vi **checkar in** klockan tre. | We are checking in at three o'clock. |
| **checka ut** | to check out | Jag **checkade ut** tidigt i morse. | I checked out early this morning. |
| **en natt** (nätter) | a night | Vi stannade tre **nätter**. | We stayed for three nights. |
| **ledig** | available | Finns det ett **ledigt** rum? | Is there a room available? |
| **fullbokad** | fully booked | Hotellet är **fullbokat** i helgen. | The hotel is fully booked this weekend. |

## Rooms and facilities

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en reception** | a reception desk | **Receptionen** är öppen hela natten. | The reception desk is open all night. |
| **ett rum** | a room | Vårt **rum** ligger på tredje våningen. | Our room is on the third floor. |
| **ett enkelrum** | a single room | Han bokade **ett enkelrum** för en natt. | He booked a single room for one night. |
| **ett dubbelrum** | a double room | Vi behöver **ett dubbelrum** med en dubbelsäng. | We need a double room with a double bed. |
| **en nyckel** | a key | Jag lämnade **nyckeln** i receptionen. | I left the key at the reception desk. |
| **ett nyckelkort** | a key card | **Nyckelkortet** öppnar dörren till rummet. | The key card opens the room door. |
| **en hiss** | an elevator | **Hissen** finns bredvid receptionen. | The elevator is next to the reception desk. |
| **en våning** | a floor, a storey | Vårt rum ligger på andra **våningen**. | Our room is on the second floor. |
| **ett badrum** | a bathroom | **Badrummet** har en dusch och en hårtork. | The bathroom has a shower and a hair dryer. |
| **en säng** | a bed | **Sängen** är bekväm. | The bed is comfortable. |
| **en dusch** | a shower | **Duschen** har varmt vatten. | The shower has hot water. |
| **en handduk** | a towel | Kan jag få en extra **handduk**? | Can I have an extra towel? |

## Hotel services

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en frukost** | a breakfast | **Frukosten** serveras från klockan sju. | Breakfast is served from seven o'clock. |
| **en frukostbuffé** | a breakfast buffet | Hotellet har **en frukostbuffé** på morgonen. | The hotel has a breakfast buffet in the morning. |
| **ingå** | to be included | Frukost **ingår** i priset. | Breakfast is included in the price. |
| **en restaurang** | a restaurant | Hotellets **restaurang** stänger klockan tio. | The hotel's restaurant closes at ten o'clock. |
| **en parkeringsplats** | a parking space | Vi bokade **en parkeringsplats** vid hotellet. | We booked a parking space at the hotel. |
