| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **väder** | weather | Det blir fint **väder** i morgon. | The weather will be nice tomorrow. |
| **ett moln** | a cloud | Ett mörkt **moln** täckte solen. | A dark cloud covered the sun. |
| **en vind** | wind | **Vinden** blåste genom träden. | The wind blew through the trees. |
| **en regnskur** | a rain shower | Efter en kort **regnskur** kom solen fram. | After a short rain shower, the sun came out. |
| **snö** | snow | **Snön** låg kvar på marken. | The snow remained on the ground. |
| **en dimma** | fog | **Dimman** gjorde det svårt att se vägen. | The fog made it hard to see the road. |
| **åska** | thunder | Vi hörde **åska** på avstånd. | We heard thunder in the distance. |
| **en blixt** | a flash of lightning | En **blixt** lyste upp himlen. | A flash of lightning lit up the sky. |
| **en hagelskur** | a hail shower | En **hagelskur** kom under eftermiddagen. | A hail shower came during the afternoon. |
| **solig** | sunny | Det var **soligt** hela dagen. | It was sunny all day. |
| **molnig** | cloudy | Himlen blev **molnig** före lunch. | The sky became cloudy before lunch. |
| **blåsig** | windy | Det var **blåsigt** vid sjön. | It was windy by the lake. |
| **regnig** | rainy | Vi hade en **regnig** helg. | We had a rainy weekend. |
| **regna** | to rain | Det **regnade** hela natten. | It rained all night. |
| **snöa** | to snow | Det **snöade** när vi åkte hem. | It was snowing when we went home. |
| **blåsa** | to blow | Det **blåste** mycket i går. | It was very windy yesterday. |
| **skina** | to shine | Solen **skiner** mellan molnen. | The sun is shining between the clouds. |
