## Movies and TV words

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en film** | a film | Vi ser **en film** hemma. | We watch a film at home. |
| **en komedi** | a comedy | Vi ser **en komedi** i kväll. | We are watching a comedy tonight. |
| **en bio** | a cinema | **Bion** ligger nära stationen. | The cinema is near the station. |
| **en tv** | a TV | Vår **tv** är liten. | Our TV is small. |
| **en tv-serie** | a TV series | Vi tittar på **tv-serien** tillsammans. | We watch the TV series together. |
| **ett tv-program** | a TV show | **Programmet** börjar klockan åtta. | The show starts at eight. |
| **ett avsnitt** | an episode | Vi ser **ett avsnitt** i kväll. | We watch an episode tonight. |
| **en kanal** | a channel | **Kanalen** visar en film. | The channel shows a film. |
| **en skådespelare** | an actor | **Skådespelaren** spelar i filmen. | The actor is in the film. |
| **en biljett** | a ticket | Jag köper **en biljett** till bion. | I buy a ticket to the cinema. |
| **en skärm** | a screen | Jag ser filmen på **skärmen**. | I watch the film on the screen. |

## Watching

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **titta på** | to watch | Vi **tittar på** en serie. | We are watching a series. |
| **se** | to watch, to see | Hon **ser** en film på tv. | She is watching a film on TV. |
| **visa** | to show | Tv:n **visar** en komedi. | The TV is showing a comedy. |
| **börja** | to begin | Filmen **börjar** klockan sju. | The film starts at seven. |
| **sluta** | to end | Programmet **slutar** klockan nio. | The show ends at nine. |
| **byta** | to change | Jag **byter** kanal nu. | I am changing the channel now. |
