# Polite phrases
*Artiga fraser*

## Goal

After this page you can thank people, make simple requests, and apologize politely.

---

## Key words

| Swedish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **tack** | thanks | **Tack** för maten! |
| **ursäkta** | excuse me | **Ursäkta**, var är stationen? |
| **förlåt** | sorry | **Förlåt**, jag kommer sent. |
| **varsågod** | here you are; you're welcome | **Varsågod**, här är din bok. |
| **gärna** | gladly; yes, please | Ja, **gärna**! |
| **hjälpa** | to help | Kan du **hjälpa** mig? |

---

## Key phrases

| Swedish | English | Use it when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Tack!** | Thank you! | Someone gives you something or helps you |
| **Tack så mycket!** | Thank you very much! | You want to thank someone warmly |
| **Varsågod!** | Here you are! / You're welcome! | You give something or reply to thanks |
| **Ursäkta!** | Excuse me! | You get someone's attention |
| **Förlåt!** | Sorry! | You apologize for a small mistake |
| **En kaffe, tack.** | A coffee, please. | You order a coffee |
| **Kan du hjälpa mig?** | Can you help me? | You ask someone for help |
| **Ja, gärna!** | Yes, please! / Gladly! | You accept an offer |

---

## Good to know

- **Requests are often short.** A friendly tone and *tack* make a simple request polite.
- **Snälla is not a direct match for “please.”** In a request, it can sound pleading or insistent.
- **Keep it simple.** *Tack* is useful in shops, cafés, and everyday situations.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tack **mycket**! | Tack **så mycket**! | The usual phrase includes *så*. |
| Jag **är förlåt**. | **Förlåt**! | *Förlåt* is not used with *är*. |
| Kan du hjälpa **jag**? | Kan du hjälpa **mig**? | Use *mig* after the verb. |

---

## Recap

- Say **Tack!** or **Tack så mycket!** to show gratitude.
- Use **Ursäkta!** to get attention and **Förlåt!** to apologize.
- Make a simple request with **En kaffe, tack** or **Kan du hjälpa mig?**
