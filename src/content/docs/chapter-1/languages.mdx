## Basic verbs

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **tala** | to speak | Jag **talar** svenska varje dag. | I speak Swedish every day. |
| **prata** | to talk, to speak | Vi **pratar** svenska tillsammans. | We speak Swedish together. |
| **förstå** | to understand | Jag **förstår** lite svenska. | I understand a little Swedish. |
| **läsa** | to read | Hon **läser** på engelska. | She reads in English. |
| **skriva** | to write | Han **skriver** på svenska. | He writes in Swedish. |
| **lyssna på** | to listen to | Jag **lyssnar på** svenska låtar. | I listen to Swedish songs. |
