# Languages
*Språk*

## Goal

After this page you can name common languages and say which languages you speak.

---

## Key words

| Swedish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **svenska** | Swedish | Jag talar **svenska**. |
| **engelska** | English | Hon talar **engelska**. |
| **finska** | Finnish | Min kompis talar **finska**. |
| **norska** | Norwegian | Ali talar **norska**. |
| **danska** | Danish | Sara talar **danska**. |
| **tyska** | German | Omar talar **tyska**. |
| **spanska** | Spanish | Vi talar **spanska**. |
| **franska** | French | De talar **franska**. |

---

## Key phrases

| Swedish | English | Use it when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Jag pratar svenska.** | I speak Swedish. | You say which language you speak |
| **Jag talar engelska.** | I speak English. | You name another language you speak |
| **Vilka språk talar du?** | Which languages do you speak? | You ask about someone's languages |
| **Talar du finska?** | Do you speak Finnish? | You ask about one language |
| **Jag pratar lite tyska.** | I speak a little German. | You describe your language ability |
| **Jag förstår norska.** | I understand Norwegian. | You say what language you understand |
| **Jag talar inte franska.** | I don't speak French. | You say you do not speak a language |

---

## Good to know

- **Many Swedish language names end in *-ska*.** For example, *svenska*, *engelska* and *franska*.
- **Both *tala* and *prata* are natural.** *Prata* is common in everyday speech; *tala* is also widely used.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Jag talar **svensk**. | Jag talar **svenska**. | *Svenska* is the language name |
| Jag talar **en svenska**. | Jag talar **svenska**. | Do not use an article before the language name here |
| Jag talar **Svenska**. | Jag talar **svenska**. | Language names are lowercase |
| Jag **är** talar engelska. | Jag talar engelska. | Do not add *är* before the present-tense verb |

---

## Recap

- Common language names include **svenska**, **engelska**, **finska** and **tyska**.
- Say **Jag talar…** or **Jag pratar…** to name a language you speak.
- Ask **Vilka språk talar du?** to ask which languages someone speaks.
