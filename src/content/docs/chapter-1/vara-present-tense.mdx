# Vara (present tense)
*Att vara i presens*

## Goal

After this page you can use the present form of *vara* in simple Swedish sentences.

---

## Overview

| Swedish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **vara → är** | to be → am, is, are | **Jag är** hemma. *(I am at home.)* |

*Use **är** with every subject pronoun.*

---

## How it works

1. **Vara becomes är in the present.** Use **är** to say who or what someone is.
   *Sara **är** student.* (Sara is a student.) · *Det **är** min bok.* (It is my book.)
2. **Är stays the same with every subject.** It does not change for one person, more than one person, or a thing.
   *Du **är** här.* (You are here.) · *De **är** hemma.* (They are at home.)
3. **Use är for a state or a place too.** It can say how someone feels or where someone or something is.
   *Ali **är** trött.* (Ali is tired.) · *Nyckeln **är** här.* (The key is here.)

---

## Good to know

- **Pronunciation varies a little by region.** The written present form is always *är*.
- **Keep the accent in är.** The letter ä is part of the correct spelling.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Jag **vara** hemma. | Jag **är** hemma. | *Vara* is the infinitive; use *är* in the present |
| Jag **har** tjugo år. | Jag **är** tjugo år. | Swedish uses *är* to say your age |
| Det **är hus**. | Det **är ett hus**. | Add *ett* before this singular noun |

---

## Recap

- The present form of **vara** is **är**.
- Use **är** with every subject; it means *am*, *is* or *are* in English.
- Use **är** for identity, a state or a place: *Jag är hemma.*
