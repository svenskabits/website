## Greetings

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **hej** | hi; hello | Jag säger **hej** till Anna. | I say hi to Anna. |
| **hallå** | hello | Jag säger **hallå** när jag svarar. | I say hello when I answer. |
| **god morgon** | good morning | Jag säger **god morgon** till Ali. | I say good morning to Ali. |
| **god kväll** | good evening | Vi säger **god kväll** till grannen. | We say good evening to the neighbor. |
| **hälsa** | to greet | Jag **hälsar** på min granne. | I greet my neighbor. |

## Goodbyes

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **hej då** | goodbye; bye | Jag säger **hej då** och går. | I say goodbye and leave. |
| **ha det bra** | take care | Jag säger **ha det bra** när hon går. | I say take care when she leaves. |
| **vi ses** | see you | **Vi ses** efter jobbet. | We will see each other after work. |
| **snart** | soon | Vi ses **snart** igen. | We will see each other again soon. |
| **god natt** | good night | Pappa säger **god natt**. | Dad says good night. |
