# Greetings and goodbyes
*Hälsa och säga hej då*

## Goal

After this page you can greet someone and say goodbye in everyday situations.

---

## Key words

| Swedish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **hälsa** | to greet | Jag **hälsar** på min granne. |
| **en hälsning** | a greeting | Hej är en vanlig **hälsning**. |
| **god morgon** | good morning | Vi säger **god morgon**. |
| **god kväll** | good evening | Vi säger **god kväll**. |
| **hej då** | goodbye | Hon säger **hej då** till Ali. |
| **ses** | see each other | Vi **ses** i morgon. |
| **snart** | soon | Vi ses **snart**. |

---

## Key phrases

| Swedish | English | Use it when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Hej!** | Hi! / Hello! | You greet someone in most situations |
| **Hallå!** | Hello! | You answer the phone or call to someone |
| **God morgon!** | Good morning! | You greet someone in the morning |
| **God kväll!** | Good evening! | You greet someone in the evening |
| **Hej då!** | Bye! | You leave someone |
| **Vi ses!** | See you! | You say goodbye to someone you will meet again |
| **Ha det bra!** | Take care! | You wish someone well as they leave |
| **God natt!** | Good night! | Someone is going to bed |

---

## Good to know

- **Hej is versatile.** It is a common, friendly greeting in everyday situations.
- **Hallå has a special use.** It is common on the phone and when you need someone's attention.
- **God natt is for bedtime.** Use *hej då* or *ha det bra* for an ordinary goodbye in the evening.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Goda** morgon! | **God** morgon! | The greeting is *god morgon*. |
| Vi ses **på snart**. | Vi ses **snart**. | *Snart* does not need *på*. |
| Jag säger **hej då** när jag kommer. | Jag säger **hej** när jag kommer. | *Hej då* is for when you leave. |

---

## Recap

- Greet someone with **Hej!** or, in the morning, **God morgon!**
- Say goodbye with **Hej då!**, **Vi ses!** or **Ha det bra!**
- Use **God natt!** when someone is going to bed.
