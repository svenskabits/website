# Å, Ä, Ö
*Å, Ä och Ö*

## Goal

After this page you can recognize and pronounce the distinct vowel sounds written å, ä and ö in common Swedish words.

---

## Overview

| What you see | What you say | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **å** | A long, rounded vowel [oː]; the tongue is toward the back | **gå** [ɡoː] *(to go)* |
| **ä** | A long, open vowel [ɛː]; the lips are unrounded | **äta** [ɛːta] *(to eat)* |
| **ö** | A long, rounded vowel [øː]; the tongue is toward the front | **öl** [øːl] *(beer)* |

*The symbol ː means that the vowel is long.*

---

## How it works

1. **Å, Ä and Ö are three different vowel sounds.** Listen for the sound quality, not just the spelling; changing the vowel can change the word.
   *gå* (to go) · *äta* (to eat) · *öl* (beer)
2. **For å, round your lips.** Keep the tongue toward the back of the mouth and make a steady vowel.
   *år* (year) · *åka* (to travel) · *gå* (to go)
3. **For ä, keep your lips unrounded.** Open your mouth and make the vowel with the tongue toward the front.
   *äta* (to eat) · *här* (here) · *läsa* (to read)
4. **For ö, round your lips with the tongue toward the front.** Keep the lips rounded, but do not move the tongue back as for å.
   *öl* (beer) · *öga* (eye) · *söt* (sweet)

---

## Words to practice

Say each word slowly. Keep the three vowel sounds distinct.

| Å | Ä | Ö |
|---|---|---|
| **gå** *(to go)* | **äta** *(to eat)* | **öl** *(beer)* |
| **år** *(year)* | **här** *(here)* | **öga** *(eye)* |
| **åka** *(to travel)* | **läsa** *(to read)* | **söt** *(sweet)* |

---

## Good to know

- **These examples use long vowels.** In other words, the same letter can have a shorter sound.
- **The exact vowel quality varies a little by region.** The three spellings still represent distinct vowels in standard Swedish.
- **The letters are not interchangeable.** A different vowel may make a different word.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **gå** said with an unrounded vowel | **gå** said with rounded lips | Å is a rounded vowel |
| **här** said with rounded lips, like **ö** | **här** said with unrounded lips | Ä and Ö are different vowels |
| **öl** said with the back-of-the-mouth vowel in **å** | **öl** said with a rounded vowel made toward the front | Ö is not Å |

---

## Recap

- **Å** is a rounded vowel made toward the back of the mouth.
- **Ä** is an open vowel made with unrounded lips.
- **Ö** is rounded, with the tongue toward the front.
- Keep **å, ä and ö** distinct when you read Swedish words.
