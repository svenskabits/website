# The Swedish alphabet
*Det svenska alfabetet*

## Goal

After this page you can name the Swedish letters and put Å, Ä and Ö in their correct place.

---

## Overview

| What you see | What you say | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **B** | *be* | **bil** *(car)* |
| **C** | *ce* | **café** *(café)* |
| **J** | *ji* | **ja** *(yes)* |
| **Q** | *ku* | **quiz** *(quiz)* |
| **W** | *dubbel-ve* | **webb** *(web)* |
| **Z** | *zäta* | **zon** *(zone)* |
| **Å, Ä, Ö** | *å, ä, ö* | **åka** *(to travel)* · **äta** *(to eat)* · **öl** *(beer)* |

---

## How it works

1. **The Swedish alphabet has 29 letters.** It has A to Z, followed by three more letters: Å, Ä and Ö.
   *zon* (zone) · *åka* (to travel) · *äta* (to eat) · *öl* (beer)
2. **Use the Swedish names when spelling aloud.** In alphabet order, the names are: *a, be, ce, de, e, eff, ge, hå, i, ji, kå, ell, em, en, o, pe, ku, ärr, ess, te, u, ve, dubbel-ve, ex, y, zäta, å, ä, ö*.
   *bil* (car) · *ja* (yes) · *webb* (web)
3. **Å, Ä and Ö are letters, not accents.** They always come after Z and keep that order.
   *zon* (zone) · *åka* (to travel) · *äta* (to eat) · *öl* (beer)
4. **Some letters are uncommon in Swedish words.** C, Q, W and Z are mostly found in names and borrowed words, but they still have Swedish letter names.
   *café* (café) · *quiz* (quiz) · *webb* (web) · *zon* (zone)

## Good to know

- **The last three have a fixed order.** Say and write them as Å, Ä, Ö.
- **C, Q, W and Z often appear in names or borrowed words.** Their Swedish letter names are still *ce, ku, dubbel-ve* and *zäta*.
- **Letter names are for spelling aloud.** Say the letter's name rather than just making its sound in a word.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **J** named with the English name “jay” | **J** named *ji* | Use the Swedish letter name, not the English one |
| **Q** named with the English name “cue” | **Q** named *ku* | The Swedish letter name is *ku* |
| **W** named “double-u” | **W** named *dubbel-ve* | The Swedish letter name is *dubbel-ve* |
| **Z** named “zee” or “zed” | **Z** named *zäta* | Use the Swedish letter name, not the English one |

---

## Recap

- The Swedish alphabet has **29 letters**: A–Z, followed by **Å, Ä, Ö**.
- Use Swedish letter names when spelling aloud, such as **J: ji**, **W: dubbel-ve** and **Z: zäta**.
- The three final letters always come in the order **Å, Ä, Ö**.
