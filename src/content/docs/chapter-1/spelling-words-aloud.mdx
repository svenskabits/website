# Spelling words aloud
*Stava ord högt*

## Goal

After this page you can spell simple Swedish words aloud using Swedish letter names.

---

## Overview

| What you see | What you say | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **SOL** | *ess · o · ell* | **sol** *(sun)* |
| **HEJ** | *hå · e · ji* | **hej** *(hello)* |
| **MAT** | *em · a · te* | **mat** *(food)* |
| **BIL** | *be · i · ell* | **bil** *(car)* |
| **TACK** | *te · a · ce · kå* | **tack** *(thanks)* |

---

## How it works

1. **Say one letter name at a time.** Follow the spelling from left to right; do not try to pronounce the whole word as you spell it.
   *sol* (sun) · *mat* (food) · *bil* (car)
2. **Use Swedish letter names.** For example, H is *hå* and J is *ji*, not their English names.
   *hej* (hello) · *ja* (yes)
3. **Say a repeated letter name twice.** Each written letter is spoken, even when two letters are the same.
   *glass* (ice cream) · *kaffe* (coffee)
4. **Pause briefly between separate words.** Keep the letters in each word together as one spelling sequence.
   *min* (my) · *bil* (car) · *tack* (thanks)

---

## Words to practice

Say the letter names in sequence, then say the word normally.

| Word | Say the letters | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **sol** | *ess · o · ell* | *(sun)* |
| **hej** | *hå · e · ji* | *(hello)* |
| **mat** | *em · a · te* | *(food)* |
| **bil** | *be · i · ell* | *(car)* |
| **tack** | *te · a · ce · kå* | *(thanks)* |
| **glass** | *ge · ell · a · ess · ess* | *(ice cream)* |
| **kaffe** | *kå · a · eff · eff · e* | *(coffee)* |
| **min** | *em · i · en* | *(my)* |

---

## Good to know

- **Letter names make the spelling clear.** They let someone write a word even if they have not heard it before.
- **A short pause helps.** Pause between words, and leave a small gap between letter names so each is clear.
- **The same letter name is used wherever the letter appears.** For example, say *ess* for each S in *glass*.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **H** named with the English name “aitch” in **hej** | **H** named *hå* | Use the Swedish letter name |
| **J** named “jay” in **hej** | **J** named *ji* | Swedish J has its own name |
| **B** named “bee” in **bil** | **B** named *be* | Use the Swedish letter name |
| One **S** name when spelling **glass** | Two *ess* names | The spelling has two S letters |

---

## Recap

- Spell from **left to right**, saying one Swedish letter name at a time.
- Use Swedish names, such as **H: hå** and **J: ji**, rather than English letter names.
- Say **repeated letters twice**, as in *glass*: *ess · ess*.
