When you spell a Swedish word aloud, say the Swedish name of each letter, in order. Do not say the sound that the letter makes inside the word.

For example, **sol** (sun) is spelled *ess – o – ell*. Say the three letter names, then say the word normally: *sol*.

## Swedish letter names

These are the usual Swedish names for all 29 letters. The written forms in the table are conventional approximations, not English pronunciation instructions. Say them naturally in Swedish. Vowel quality and some letter names can vary a little by speaker and region; **R** (*ärr*) is one name whose sound may be noticeably different.

| A | *a* |
| B | *be* |
| C | *se* |
| D | *de* |
| E | *e* |
| F | *eff* |
| G | *ge* |
| H | *hå* |
| I | *i* |
| J | *ji* |
| K | *kå* |
| L | *ell* |
| M | *emm* |
| N | *enn* |
| O | *o* |
| P | *pe* |
| Q | *ku* |
| R | *ärr* |
| S | *ess* |

The extra Swedish letters Å, Ä and Ö each have their own name. They come after Z in the alphabet, and you say the name of the exact letter in the word.

## Spell from left to right

Say one name for every written letter. A brief beat between names helps the listener follow; keep the letters in the same word together as one sequence. If you spell a phrase with more than one word, pause briefly between the words.

| Word | Say the letters | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *hej* | *hå – e – ji* | hello |
| *mat* | *emm – a – te* | food |
| *bil* | *be – i – ell* | car |
| *tack* | *te – a – se – kå* | thanks |
| *öl* | *ö – ell* | beer |
| *är* | *ä – ärr* | is; are |
| *år* | *å – ärr* | year |

The name of a letter is not necessarily the sound it represents in a word. For example, the name of **H** is *hå*, but when you spell *hej*, you say the names *hå – e – ji*; you do not pronounce the whole word until the spelling is finished. A letter can also have different sounds in different words, so spelling and reading aloud are different tasks.

## Say every repeated letter

When the same letter appears twice, say its name twice. Do not leave one out.

| Word | Say the letters | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *glass* | *ge – ell – a – ess – ess* | ice cream |
| *glas* | *ge – ell – a – ess* | glass (drinking vessel) |
| *kaffe* | *kå – a – eff – eff – e* | coffee |
| *Anna* | *a – enn – enn – a* | Anna (a name) |

If the listener misses the double letter, clarify that the letter occurs twice: there are two *ess* letters. Then continue with the remaining letters if needed.

## Spell a name or a word

You may hear **Hur stavar du ditt namn?** (How do you spell your name?) You can answer by saying each letter name in sequence. For example:

> **Anna:** *a – enn – enn – a*

If someone does not hear a letter clearly, repeat that letter name or give a familiar example word that starts with it. Speak clearly, especially when the names are close together in fast speech. Uppercase and lowercase forms have the same letter name.

Remember: follow the written word from left to right, use Swedish letter names, and say a name for every letter.
