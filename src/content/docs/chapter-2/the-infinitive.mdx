# The infinitive
*Infinitiv*

## Goal

After this page you can use the infinitive form of a Swedish verb in a simple sentence.

---

## Overview

| Infinitive | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **bo** | to live | Jag vill **bo** här. *(I want to live here.)* |
| **läsa** | to read | Ali tycker om att **läsa**. *(Ali likes to read.)* |
| **vara** | to be | Sara vill **vara** hemma. *(Sara wants to be at home.)* |
| **gå** | to go | Vi kan **gå** hem. *(We can go home.)* |

---

## How it works

1. **The infinitive is the basic form of a verb.** In English, it often means “to” do something.
   *Att **bo** här är bra.* (To live here is good.)
2. **Many infinitives end in -a, but not all.** Common infinitives without -a include *bo*, *gå* and *se*.
   *Ali vill **läsa**. · Sara kan **gå**.* (Ali wants to read. · Sara can go.)
3. **Some phrases use att before the infinitive.** The verb stays in its basic form after *att*.
   *Jag tycker om att **läsa**.* (I like to read.) · *Det är bra att **vila**.* (It is good to rest.)
4. **After kan and vill, use the infinitive without att.**
   *Vi kan **bo** här.* (We can live here.) · *Ali vill **gå** hem.* (Ali wants to go home.)

---

## Good to know

- **Dictionaries may show att with the infinitive.** *Bo* and *att bo* both show the same verb form.
- **The present form is different.** Many verbs add *-r*: *bo* becomes *bor* in *Jag bor här.* (I live here.)

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Jag vill **bor** här. | Jag vill **bo** här. | Use the infinitive after *vill*. |
| Jag tycker om att **läser**. | Jag tycker om att **läsa**. | Use the infinitive after *att*. |
| Vi kan **att gå** hem. | Vi kan **gå** hem. | Do not use *att* after *kan*. |
| Jag **bo** här. | Jag **bor** här. | Use the present form for a simple statement. |

---

## Recap

- The infinitive is the basic verb form: **bo, läsa, vara, gå**.
- Many infinitives end in **-a**, but some common ones do not.
- Some phrases use **att + infinitive**; after **kan** and **vill**, use the infinitive without *att*.
