# Phone numbers
*Telefonnummer*

## Goal

After this page you can ask for, say, and write down a phone number.

---

## Key words

| Swedish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **ett telefonnummer** | a phone number | Mitt **telefonnummer** är nytt. |
| **ett mobilnummer** | a mobile number | Jag har ett **mobilnummer**. |
| **en siffra** | a digit | **Siffran** är sju. |
| **ringa** | to call | Jag **ringer** min vän. |
| **upprepa** | to repeat | Kan du **upprepa** numret? |
| **skriva ner** | to write down | Jag **skriver ner** numret. |

---

## Key phrases

| Swedish | English | Use it when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Vad är ditt telefonnummer?** | What's your phone number? | You ask for a phone number |
| **Vad är ditt mobilnummer?** | What's your mobile number? | You ask for someone's mobile number |
| **Mitt nummer är …** | My number is … | You give your number |
| **Kan du upprepa numret?** | Can you repeat the number? | You did not hear it clearly |
| **Kan du säga numret långsamt?** | Can you say the number slowly? | You need more time to write it down |
| **Jag skriver ner det.** | I'll write it down. | You take note of a number |
| **Jag ringer dig senare.** | I'll call you later. | You say when you will call |

---

## Good to know

- **Say digits clearly.** You can say them one by one or in small groups, with a short pause between groups.
- **Use *nummer* for short.** In context, *Vad är ditt nummer?* can mean “What's your phone number?”
- **Write it clearly.** Spaces or hyphens can separate groups of digits in writing.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Vad är **min** telefonnummer? | Vad är **mitt** telefonnummer? | *Telefonnummer* is an ett-word. |
| ett **telefon nummer** | ett **telefonnummer** | Write this compound as one word. |
| Jag **ringa** dig senare. | Jag **ringer** dig senare. | Use *ringer* with *jag*. |

---

## Recap

- Ask for a number with **Vad är ditt telefonnummer?**
- Ask someone to repeat it: **Kan du upprepa numret?**
- Use **skriva ner** to write down a number and **ringa** to call.
