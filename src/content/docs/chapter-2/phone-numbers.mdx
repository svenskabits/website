## Phone words

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en telefon** | a phone | **Telefonen** står på bordet. | The phone is on the table. |
| **en mobil** | a mobile phone | Min **mobil** ligger här. | My mobile phone is here. |
| **ett nummer** | a number | Jag hittar **numret** i mobilen. | I find the number in my mobile phone. |
| **ett telefonnummer** | a phone number | Jag sparar **ett telefonnummer** i mobilen. | I save a phone number in my mobile phone. |
| **ett mobilnummer** | a mobile number | Jag skriver **ett mobilnummer** här. | I write a mobile number here. |
| **en siffra** | a digit | Jag ser **siffran** på skärmen. | I see the digit on the screen. |

## Phone actions

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ringa** | to call | Jag **ringer** min vän nu. | I am calling my friend now. |
| **svara** | to answer | Hon **svarar** i telefonen. | She answers the phone. |
| **säga** | to say | Han **säger** sitt nummer tydligt. | He says his number clearly. |
| **upprepa** | to repeat | Jag **upprepar** numret långsamt. | I repeat the number slowly. |
| **skriva ner** | to write down | Jag **skriver ner** numret. | I write down the number. |
| **spara** | to save | Jag **sparar** numret i mobilen. | I save the number in my mobile phone. |
| **läsa** | to read | Hon **läser** numret högt. | She reads the number aloud. |
