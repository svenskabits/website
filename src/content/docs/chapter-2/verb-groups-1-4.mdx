# Verb groups 1–4
*Verbgrupperna 1–4*

## Goal

After this page you can use common present-tense forms from Swedish verb groups 1–4.

---

## Overview

| Group | Infinitive → present | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **tala → talar** | Jag **talar** svenska. *(I speak Swedish.)* |
| 2 | **läsa → läser** | Ali **läser** en bok. *(Ali reads a book.)* |
| 3 | **bo → bor** | Vi **bor** här. *(We live here.)* |
| 4 | **skriva → skriver** | Sara **skriver** en bok. *(Sara writes a book.)* |

---

## How it works

1. **Group 1 uses -ar in the present.** Many infinitives in this group end in *-a*; change the ending to *-ar*.
   *Jag **pratar** svenska.* (I speak Swedish.)
2. **Group 2 uses -er in the present.** Its infinitives also often end in *-a*.
   *Ali **köper** en bil.* (Ali buys a car.)
3. **Group 3 adds -r to the infinitive.** These infinitives do not end in *-a*.
   *Vi **tror** på Anna.* (We believe in Anna.)
4. **Group 4 has many common verbs to learn as pairs.** Their present forms often end in *-er*.
   *Sara **dricker** vatten.* (Sara drinks water.)

---

## Good to know

- **Groups 2 and 4 can look alike in the present.** Both can have a present form ending in *-er*, so learn each verb with its present form.
- **The verb form is the same with every subject.** Say *jag talar*, *du talar* and *vi talar*.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Jag **prata** svenska. | Jag **pratar** svenska. | Group 1 uses *-ar* in the present. |
| Ali **läsar** en bok. | Ali **läser** en bok. | Group 2 uses *-er* in the present. |
| Vi **bo** här. | Vi **bor** här. | Group 3 adds *-r*. |
| Sara **skriv** en bok. | Sara **skriver** en bok. | Use the present form of *skriva*. |

---

## Recap

- Group 1: **-ar**; group 2: **-er**.
- Group 3 adds **-r** to the infinitive.
- Many group 4 present forms end in **-er**; learn common verbs as pairs, such as **skriva → skriver**.
