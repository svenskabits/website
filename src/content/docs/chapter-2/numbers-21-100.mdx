# Numbers 21–100
*Talen 21–100*

## Goal

After this page you can count to one hundred and say common numbers, ages, and prices.

---

## Key words

| Swedish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **tjugo** | twenty | De är **tjugo** personer. |
| **tjugoett** | twenty-one | Hon är **tjugoett** år. |
| **trettio** | thirty | Boken kostar **trettio** kronor. |
| **fyrtio** | forty | Han är **fyrtio** år. |
| **femtio** | fifty | **Femtio** personer väntar. |
| **sextio** | sixty | Bussen går om **sextio** minuter. |
| **sjuttio** | seventy | Det kostar **sjuttio** kronor. |
| **åttio** | eighty | Min mormor är **åttio** år. |
| **nittio** | ninety | Det kostar **nittio** kronor. |
| **hundra** | one hundred | Jag räknar till **hundra**. |

---

## Key phrases

| Swedish | English | Use it when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Jag är tjugofem år.** | I am twenty-five years old. | You say your age |
| **Det kostar trettio kronor.** | It costs thirty kronor. | You say a price |
| **Vi ses klockan tjugoett.** | See you at nine p.m. | You arrange a time |
| **Jag fyller fyrtio i år.** | I turn forty this year. | You talk about your birthday |
| **Nummer fyrtiofem, tack.** | Number forty-five, please. | You give or ask for a number |

---

## Good to know

- **Build numbers from 21 to 99.** Say the tens first, then the ones: *trettiofyra* (34), *sextioåtta* (68).
- **Write compound numbers as one word.** For example, *tjugotre* (23) and *nittiosex* (96).
- **Learn the tens.** The pattern starts with *tjugo, trettio, fyrtio,* and continues to *nittio*.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **tjugo en** | **tjugoett** | Compound numbers are one word. |
| **trettio två** | **trettiotvå** | Write the tens and ones together. |
| **fyrti** | **fyrtio** | The word for forty ends in *-tio*. |
| **sjutio** | **sjuttio** | *Sjuttio* has two t's. |

---

## Recap

- Learn the tens: **trettio, fyrtio, femtio … nittio**.
- Make numbers like 34 and 68 with one word: **trettiofyra**, **sextioåtta**.
- Use number words for ages and prices: **Jag är tjugofem år.**, **Det kostar trettio kronor.**
