# Places in town
*Platser i staden*

## Goal

After this page you can name common places in town and ask where they are.

---

## Key words

| Swedish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **en mataffär** | a grocery store | Jag handlar i **mataffären**. |
| **ett apotek** | a pharmacy | **Apoteket** säljer medicin. |
| **en busshållplats** | a bus stop | Bussen stannar vid **busshållplatsen**. |
| **en station** | a station | **Stationen** ligger nära centrum. |
| **ett bibliotek** | a library | Jag lånar böcker på **biblioteket**. |
| **en vårdcentral** | a health centre | **Vårdcentralen** öppnar klockan åtta. |
| **ett torg** | a town square | Vi sitter på **torget**. |

---

## Key phrases

| Swedish | English | Use it when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Var ligger apoteket?** | Where is the pharmacy? | You ask for a place |
| **Var finns en mataffär?** | Where is there a grocery store? | You look for a store |
| **Finns det ett bibliotek här?** | Is there a library here? | You ask if a place is nearby |
| **Det ligger nära stationen.** | It's near the station. | You describe where something is |
| **Det ligger i centrum.** | It's in the town centre. | You give a general location |
| **Ligger busshållplatsen långt härifrån?** | Is the bus stop far from here? | You ask about distance |
| **Jag letar efter vårdcentralen.** | I'm looking for the health centre. | You need to find a place |

---

## Good to know

- **Centrum means town centre.** It is a useful word in both small towns and cities.
- **Use the definite form for a known place.** *Apoteket* means “the pharmacy”; *ett apotek* means “a pharmacy.”
- **Many places have service information online.** Search for the place name and the town name.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **En** apotek | **Ett** apotek | *Apotek* is an ett-word. |
| **En** bibliotek | **Ett** bibliotek | *Bibliotek* is an ett-word. |
| Var apoteket **ligger**? | Var **ligger** apoteket? | The verb comes before the subject in a question. |

---

## Recap

- Name places like **en mataffär**, **ett apotek** and **ett bibliotek**.
- Ask where a place is: **Var ligger apoteket?**
- Ask if a place is nearby: **Finns det ett bibliotek här?**
