| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en mataffär** | a grocery store | Jag handlar i **mataffären**. | I shop at the grocery store. |
| **en butik** | a shop | **Butiken** öppnar klockan nio. | The shop opens at nine. |
| **ett apotek** | a pharmacy | **Apoteket** säljer medicin. | The pharmacy sells medicine. |
| **en bank** | a bank | **Banken** ligger vid torget. | The bank is by the square. |
| **en restaurang** | a restaurant | Vi äter på **restaurangen**. | We eat at the restaurant. |
| **ett café** | a café | Vi dricker kaffe på **caféet**. | We drink coffee at the café. |
| **ett bibliotek** | a library | Jag lånar böcker på **biblioteket**. | I borrow books at the library. |
| **en skola** | a school | Barnen går till **skolan**. | The children go to school. |
| **en vårdcentral** | a health centre | Jag ringer **vårdcentralen**. | I call the health centre. |
| **en busshållplats** | a bus stop | Bussen stannar vid **busshållplatsen**. | The bus stops at the bus stop. |
| **en station** | a station | Tåget går från **stationen**. | The train leaves from the station. |
| **ett torg** | a square | Vi ses på **torget**. | We meet at the square. |
| **en park** | a park | Barnen leker i **parken**. | The children play in the park. |
| **ett hotell** | a hotel | Vi bor på **hotellet**. | We are staying at the hotel. |
| **en toalett** | a toilet | Var är **toaletten**? | Where is the toilet? |
| **ett centrum** | a town centre | **Centrum** ligger nära stationen. | The town centre is near the station. |
| **en biograf** | a cinema | **Biografen** visar en film. | The cinema is showing a film. |
