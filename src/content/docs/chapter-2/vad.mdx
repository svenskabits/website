# Vad
*Vad*

## Goal

After this page you can ask what a person does, has or wants.

---

## Overview

```text
Vad + verb + subject?
```

- **Vad** heter du? *(What is your name?)*
- **Vad** äter Ali? *(What is Ali eating?)*
- **Vad** gör Sara? *(What is Sara doing?)*

---

## How it works

1. **Vad asks about a thing or information.** Use it to ask what someone has, eats or does.
   *Vad **har** Anna?* (What does Anna have?)
2. **Put the verb before the subject.** In a direct question, say *vad*, then the verb, then the person.
   *Vad **gillar** du?* (What do you like?)
3. **Use vad to ask someone's name.** The usual question is *Vad heter du?*
   *Vad **heter** Ali?* (What is Ali's name?)
4. **Vad can be the subject.** When you ask what happens, there is no person after the verb.
   *Vad **händer**?* (What is happening?)

---

## Good to know

- **Vad is often pronounced “va” in speech.** It is still written *vad*.
- **A short answer is enough.** *Vad äter du?* can have the answer *En smörgås.* (A sandwich.)

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Vem** heter du? | **Vad** heter du? | Use *vad* to ask what someone's name is. |
| Vad **du äter**? | Vad **äter du**? | In a direct question, put the verb before the subject. |
| **Vad** bor du? | **Var** bor du? | Use *var* to ask about a place. |

---

## Recap

- **Vad** asks about a thing, an activity or information.
- In a direct question, use **vad + verb + subject**: *Vad äter du?*
- Use **Vad heter du?** to ask someone's name.
- **Vad händer?** asks what is happening; *vad* is the subject.
