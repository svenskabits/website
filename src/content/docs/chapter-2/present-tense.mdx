# Present tense
*Presens*

## Goal

After this page you can use common Swedish verbs to talk about now and everyday life.

---

## Overview

| Infinitive | Present | Example |
|---|---|---|
| bo (live) | **bor** | Jag **bor** här. *(I live here.)* |
| prata (speak) | **pratar** | Anna **pratar** svenska. *(Anna speaks Swedish.)* |
| läsa (read) | **läser** | Ali **läser** en bok. *(Ali reads a book.)* |
| vara (be) | **är** | Vi **är** hemma. *(We are at home.)* |
| ha (have) | **har** | Maria **har** en bil. *(Maria has a car.)* |

---

## How it works

1. **The verb stays the same for each subject.** Use the same present form with *jag, du, hon,* or *vi*.
   *Jag **bor** här.* (I live here.) · *Du **bor** här.* (You live here.)
2. **Many present forms end in -ar.** For example, *prata* has the present form **pratar**.
   *Vi **pratar** svenska.* (We speak Swedish.)
3. **Some common verbs have special forms.** Learn forms such as *vara* → **är** and *ha* → **har**.
   *Han **är** hemma.* (He is at home.) · *De **har** en bil.* (They have a car.)
4. **Use the present for now or for things that happen regularly.**
   *Ali **läser** nu.* (Ali is reading now.) · *Ali **läser** varje dag.* (Ali reads every day.)

---

## Good to know

- **Do not translate “do” or “does” word for word in a simple statement.** Swedish uses the main verb: *Jag arbetar.* (I work.)
- **English often uses “am/is/are + -ing.”** Swedish still uses its present form: *Jag arbetar nu.* (I am working now.)

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Jag **bo** här. | Jag **bor** här. | Use the present form, not the infinitive. |
| Hon **läs** en bok. | Hon **läser** en bok. | The present form of *läsa* is *läser*. |
| Vi **vara** hemma. | Vi **är** hemma. | *Vara* is the infinitive; the present form is *är*. |
| Jag **är läser** nu. | Jag **läser** nu. | Use one present verb here. |

---

## Recap

- A present-tense verb has the same form with **jag, du, hon** and **vi**.
- Many verbs have present forms ending in **-ar** or **-er**.
- Learn common forms such as **är** (*vara*) and **har** (*ha*).
- Use the present for **now** and for things that happen **regularly**.
