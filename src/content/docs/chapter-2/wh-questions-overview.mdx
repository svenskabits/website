# Wh-questions overview
*Frågeord*

## Goal

After this page you can choose **var, vad, vem** or **hur** to ask a simple question.

---

## Overview

| Swedish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **var** | where | **Var** bor Ali? *(Where does Ali live?)* |
| **vad** | what | **Vad** heter Sara? *(What is Sara's name?)* |
| **vem** | who | **Vem** är läraren? *(Who is the teacher?)* |
| **hur** | how | **Hur** mår Ali? *(How is Ali?)* |

---

## How it works

1. **Use var for a place.** Ask where a person or thing is.
   *Var **bor** Ali?* (Where does Ali live?)
2. **Use vad for a thing or name.**
   *Vad **äter** Sara?* (What is Sara eating?)
3. **Use vem for a person.**
   * **Vem** är läraren?* (Who is the teacher?)
4. **Use hur to ask about the way or condition.**
   *Hur **mår** du?* (How are you?)
5. **Put the verb before the subject.** In a direct question, the word order is question word + verb + subject.
   *Var **bor** Ali?* (Where does Ali live?) · *Vad **heter** Sara?* (What is Sara's name?)

---

## Good to know

- **Vad heter du? is the usual question for a person's name.** The answer is *Jag heter …* (My name is …).
- **Hur mår du? is a common greeting.** It asks how someone feels.

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Var** heter du? | **Vad** heter du? | Use *vad* to ask someone's name. |
| **Vad** bor Ali? | **Var** bor Ali? | Use *var* to ask about a place. |
| Vem bor Ali? | Vem bor Ali **med**? | Add *med* to ask who Ali lives with. |
| Vad **du** heter? | Vad **heter** du? | In a direct question, put the verb before the subject. |

---

## Recap

- Use **var** for a place, **vad** for a thing or name, **vem** for a person and **hur** for a way or condition.
- In a direct question, put the **verb before the subject**: *Var bor Ali?*
- **Vad heter du?** asks for a name. **Hur mår du?** asks how someone feels.
