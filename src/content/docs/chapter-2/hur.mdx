# Hur
*Hur*

## Goal

After this page you can ask how someone feels or how someone does something.

---

## Overview

```text
Hur + verb + subject + rest?
Hur + description word + verb + subject?
```

- **Hur** mår du? *(How are you?)*
- **Hur** bor Anna? *(How does Anna live?)*
- **Hur gammal** är Ali? *(How old is Ali?)*

---

## How it works

1. **Hur asks about the way or condition.** It often means “how” in English.
   *Hur **mår** du?* (How are you?) · *Hur **går** det?* (How is it going?)
2. **Put the verb before the subject.** In a direct question, say *hur*, then the verb, then the person.
   *Hur **bor du**?* (How do you live?) · *Hur **kommer** Ali hit?* (How does Ali get here?)
3. **Put a description word after hur.** For questions like *how old* or *how tired*, put the description word before the verb.
   *Hur **gammal är** du?* (How old are you?) · *Hur **trött är** Anna?* (How tired is Anna?)

---

## Good to know

- **Hur mycket asks about an amount or price.** *Hur mycket kostar det?* (How much does it cost?)
- **Hur är vädret? asks about the weather.** It means “How is the weather?”

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Hur du mår**? | **Hur mår du**? | Put the verb before the subject in a direct question. |
| **Hur du bor**? | **Hur bor du**? | Put the verb before the subject in a direct question. |
| **Hur gammal du är**? | **Hur gammal är du**? | Put the verb before the subject. |

---

## Recap

- **Hur** asks about how something is or happens.
- In a direct question, use **Hur + verb + subject**: *Hur mår du?*
- With a description word, use **Hur + description word + verb + subject**: *Hur gammal är du?*
- **Hur mycket** asks about an amount or price.
