# Var
*Var*

## Goal

After this page you can ask where a person or thing is.

---

## Overview

```text
Var + verb + subject?
```

- **Var** bor du? *(Where do you live?)*
- **Var** är Anna? *(Where is Anna?)*
- **Var** ligger skolan? *(Where is the school?)*

---

## How it works

1. **Var asks about a place.** Use it to ask where someone or something is.
   *Var **är** Ali?* (Where is Ali?)
2. **Put the verb before the subject.** After *var*, say the verb and then the person or thing.
   *Var **bor** Maria?* (Where does Maria live?)
3. **Answer with the place.** A short answer can be just a place name or a place word.
   *Var bor du? – **I Malmö**.* (Where do you live? – In Malmö.)

---

## Good to know

- **Use a place word in the answer.** For example, *hemma* means “at home” and *i Stockholm* means “in Stockholm”.
- **The question word stays the same.** It does not change when you talk to one person or a group: *Var bor du?* (Where do you live?) · *Var bor ni?* (Where do you live? [to a group])

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Vad** bor du? | **Var** bor du? | *Vad* asks what; *var* asks about a place. |
| **Var du** bor? | **Var bor du**? | In a direct question, put the verb before the subject. |
| Vet du var **bor Anna**? | Vet du var **Anna bor**? | In this question inside a sentence, put the subject before the verb. |

---

## Recap

- **Var** asks about a place.
- In a direct question, use **var + verb + subject**: *Var bor du?*
- Answer with a **place**: *I Malmö*, *hemma*.
