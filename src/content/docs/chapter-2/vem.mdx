# Vem
*Vem*

## Goal

After this page you can ask who a person is or who does something.

---

## Overview

```text
Vem + verb + rest?
```

- **Vem** är hon? *(Who is she?)*
- **Vem** ser du? *(Who do you see?)*
- **Vem** bor här? *(Who lives here?)*

---

## How it works

1. **Vem asks about a person.** Use it when you want to know which person.
   *Vem **är** Ali?* (Who is Ali?)
2. **Put the verb after vem.** In a direct question, say *vem*, then the verb.
   *Vem **ser** du?* (Who do you see?) · *Vem **är** hon?* (Who is she?)
3. **Vem can be the subject.** When you ask who does something, *vem* is followed by the verb and no other person.
   *Vem **bor** här?* (Who lives here?) · *Vem **kommer** idag?* (Who is coming today?)

---

## Good to know

- **Use vad to ask someone's name.** Say *Vad heter du?* (What is your name?), not *Vem heter du?*
- **A short answer is fine.** *Vem är hon?* can have the answer *Anna.* (Anna.)

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Vem **du är**? | Vem **är du**? | In a direct question, put the verb before the subject. |
| **Vem** heter du? | **Vad** heter du? | Use *vad* to ask someone's name. |
| **Vem** bor du? | **Var** bor du? | Use *var* to ask about a place. |
| **vem** är du? | **Vem** är du? | Capitalize the first word of a sentence. |

---

## Recap

- **Vem** asks about a person.
- In a direct question, put the verb after **vem**: *Vem ser du?*
- **Vem** can be the subject: *Vem bor här?*
- Ask for someone's name with **Vad heter du?**
