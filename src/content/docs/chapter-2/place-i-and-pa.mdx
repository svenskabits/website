# Place: i and på
*Prepositionerna i och på för plats*

## Goal

After this page you can use **i** and **på** to say where a person or thing is.

---

## Overview

| Swedish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **i + stad/land** | in a city/country | Ali bor **i** Stockholm. *(Ali lives in Stockholm.)* |
| **i + rum/väska** | in a room/bag | Nyckeln ligger **i** väskan. *(The key is in the bag.)* |
| **på + plats** | at certain places | Sara är **på** jobbet. *(Sara is at work.)* |
| **på + yta** | on a surface | Boken ligger **på** bordet. *(The book is on the table.)* |

---

## How it works

1. **Use i with cities and countries.** Say where you live with *i*.
   *Ali bor **i** Sverige.* (Ali lives in Sweden.)
2. **Use i for being inside a room or container.**
   *Maten är **i** kylskåpet.* (The food is in the fridge.)
3. **Use på in common place expressions.** Say *på jobbet*, *på biblioteket* and *på restaurang*.
   *Sara är **på** biblioteket.* (Sara is at the library.)
4. **Use på for something on a surface.**
   *Nyckeln ligger **på** bordet.* (The key is on the table.)

---

## Good to know

- **Some place expressions are fixed.** Learn the whole expression, such as *på jobbet* or *på restaurang*.
- **På does not always mean “on top of.”** *På biblioteket* means “at the library.”

---

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Jag bor **på** Stockholm. | Jag bor **i** Stockholm. | Use *i* with city names. |
| Jag är **på** köket. | Jag är **i** köket. | Use *i* for being inside a room. |
| Vi äter **i** restaurang. | Vi äter **på** restaurang. | The expression is *på restaurang*. |
| Boken ligger **i** bordet. | Boken ligger **på** bordet. | Use *på* for a surface. |

---

## Recap

- Use **i** with cities, countries, rooms and containers.
- Use **på** in common expressions like **på jobbet** and **på biblioteket**.
- Use **på** for something on a surface, like **på bordet**.
