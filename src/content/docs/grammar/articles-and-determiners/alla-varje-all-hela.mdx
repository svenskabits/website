---
title: All and Every
description: Learn when to use varje, alla, all, allt, and hela to talk about individuals, groups, amounts, and whole things.
---

Swedish uses different words for **each or every** individual, **all** members of a group, and **the whole** of one thing. They are not interchangeable.

## Each or every: varje

Use **varje** before a singular noun when you mean each individual one or every occurrence. The noun stays singular, and the verb agrees with that singular noun.

- **varje dag** — every day
- **varje barn** — each child
- **Varje elev har en bok.** — Every student has a book.

## All members: alla

Use **alla** with a plural noun to refer to the group as a whole. You can also use it on its own to mean **everyone** or **all of them**.

- **alla elever** — all the students
- **Alla eleverna är här.** — All the students are here.
- **Alla kom.** — Everyone came.

## All of a quantity or group: all, allt, alla

When **all** means the complete amount or group, its form matches the noun:

| Form | Goes with | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **all** | an *en*-word, including an *en*-gender mass noun | **all mjölk** — all milk |
| **allt** | an *ett*-word, including an *ett*-gender mass noun | **allt vatten** — all water |
| **alla** | a plural noun | **alla böcker** — all books |

When you mean a specific, already-known amount or group, the noun can also be definite: **all mjölken** (“all the milk”), **allt vattnet** (“all the water”), **alla böckerna** (“all the books”).

## The whole or entire: hela

Use **hela** with a definite singular noun to mean the whole or entire thing, often a period of time or a physical object: **hela dagen** (“the whole day”), **hela huset** (“the whole house”).

- **Jag arbetade hela dagen.** — I worked all day.
- **Vi såg hela filmen.** — We watched the whole film.

## Common confusion

**Varje** and **alla** do not mean the same thing: **varje elev** looks at each student individually; **alla elever** refers to the students as a group. Likewise, **hela dagen** means one complete day, while **varje dag** means every day.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Jag tar bussen varje dag.** — I take the bus every day.
2. **Varje barn får en biljett.** — Each child gets a ticket.
3. **Alla eleverna är här.** — All the students are here.
4. **Alla kom i tid.** — Everyone arrived on time.
5. **All mjölken är slut.** — All the milk is gone.
6. **Allt vattnet är borta.** — All the water is gone.
7. **Alla böckerna ligger på bordet.** — All the books are on the table.
8. **Vi stannade hemma hela dagen.** — We stayed home all day.
9. **Hon läste hela boken.** — She read the whole book.
10. **Butiken är öppen varje lördag.** — The shop is open every Saturday.
