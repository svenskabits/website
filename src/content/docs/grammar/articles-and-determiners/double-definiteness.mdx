title: Double Definiteness
description: Learn how Swedish marks a definite noun with a preceding adjective using both a preposed article and a definite noun ending.
---

## The pattern

When a definite noun has an adjective before it, Swedish normally uses **two** definiteness markers: **den**, **det**, or **de** before the adjective, and a definite ending on the noun. The adjective takes its definite form, which often ends in **-a**:

| Noun type | Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| En-word, singular | **den** + adjective + definite noun | **den stora bilen** — the big car |
| Ett-word, singular | **det** + adjective + definite noun | **det stora huset** — the big house |
| Plural | **de** + adjective + definite noun | **de stora bilarna** — the big cars |

The adjective form is not always a regular **-a** form. For example, **liten** becomes **lilla** in **den lilla bilen** (“the small car”). Learn the adjective form with the phrase when it is irregular.

## Examples

- **Den röda bilen står utanför.** — The red car is outside.
- **Det gamla huset ligger vid sjön.** — The old house is by the lake.
- **De nya bilarna är dyra.** — The new cars are expensive.
- **Jag gillar den varma maten.** — I like the warm food.
- **Vi såg det höga berget.** — We saw the tall mountain.
- **Barnen leker i den stora trädgården.** — The children are playing in the big garden.

## Compare the forms

Without an adjective, the definite noun normally stands by itself: **bilen** (“the car”), **huset** (“the house”), **bilarna** (“the cars”). Add both the preposed article and the definite noun form when an adjective comes first: **den stora bilen**, **det stora huset**, **de stora bilarna**.

An indefinite noun uses a different pattern: **en stor bil** means “a big car.” Do not combine its indefinite article and adjective with a definite noun ending: say **en stor bil** or **den stora bilen**, not *en stor bilen*.

## Common confusion

The **den/det/de** in this pattern agrees with the noun, not the adjective: **den stora bilen**, but **det stora huset**. Also, **den bilen** without an adjective can mean “that car”; for the ordinary meaning “the car,” say **bilen**.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Den svarta katten sover.** — The black cat is sleeping.
2. **Det lilla barnet skrattar.** — The little child is laughing.
3. **De röda äpplena är söta.** — The red apples are sweet.
4. **Jag stängde den öppna dörren.** — I closed the open door.
5. **Hon bor i det gula huset.** — She lives in the yellow house.
6. **Vi väntar på den sena bussen.** — We are waiting for the late bus.
7. **De gamla böckerna står här.** — The old books are here.
8. **Han köpte den blå jackan.** — He bought the blue jacket.
9. **Det varma kaffet står på bordet.** — The warm coffee is on the table.
10. **De små hundarna springer fort.** — The small dogs run fast.
