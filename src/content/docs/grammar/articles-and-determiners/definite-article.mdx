## Two ways to mark definiteness

English usually puts **the** before a noun. Swedish commonly marks a definite noun by adding an ending:

- **en bil** (a car) → **bilen** (the car)
- **ett hus** (a house) → **huset** (the house)
- **bilar** (cars) → **bilarna** (the cars)

For singular nouns, en-words commonly take **-en** or **-n**, and ett-words take **-et** or **-t**. Definite plural endings depend on the plural form: **-na** and **-a** are common, and some plurals take **-en**, as in **husen** (the houses).

When an adjective comes before a definite noun, Swedish also uses a word before the adjective and noun: **den** with an en-word, **det** with an ett-word, and **de** with a plural noun. For example, **den röda bilen** means “the red car.” The noun keeps its definite ending. This combined pattern is sometimes called *double definiteness*; it is covered in more detail in the next lesson.

## Examples

- **Bilen står utanför.** — The car is outside.
- **Huset är gammalt.** — The house is old.
- **Bilarna är nya.** — The cars are new.
- **Den röda bilen är min.** — The red car is mine.
- **Det stora huset ligger vid sjön.** — The big house is by the lake.
- **De gamla bilarna är dyra.** — The old cars are expensive.

## Common confusion

For an ordinary definite noun without an adjective, use the ending without **den**, **det**, or **de**: **bilen**, not *den bilen*. (The phrase **den bilen** can be used with a different meaning, “that car.”) With an adjective before the noun, use both: **den röda bilen**, not *den röda bil*.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Katten sover.** — The cat is sleeping.
2. **Jag låser bilen.** — I lock the car.
3. **Huset är varmt.** — The house is warm.
4. **Var är nyckeln?** — Where is the key?
5. **Böckerna ligger på bordet.** — The books are on the table.
6. **Barnen leker ute.** — The children are playing outside.
7. **Flickorna går till skolan.** — The girls are walking to school.
8. **Den blå cykeln är min.** — The blue bicycle is mine.
9. **Det lilla äpplet är sött.** — The little apple is sweet.
10. **De nya skorna är bekväma.** — The new shoes are comfortable.
