## The definite plural

Use the definite plural when you mean **the** things or people in a group. Swedish usually marks this by adding an ending to the plural noun, rather than by putting a separate word for **the** before it:

- **bilar** — cars → **bilarna** — the cars
- **hus** — houses → **husen** — the houses

The definite plural is different from the definite singular: **bilen** means “the car,” while **bilarna** means “the cars.”

## Choosing the ending

The ending depends on the noun’s plural pattern. These common examples show how the indefinite plural changes:

| Singular | Indefinite plural | Definite plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| en flicka (a girl) | flickor (girls) | flickorna (the girls) |
| en bil (a car) | bilar (cars) | bilarna (the cars) |
| en katt (a cat) | katter (cats) | katterna (the cats) |
| ett äpple (an apple) | äpplen (apples) | äpplena (the apples) |
| ett hus (a house) | hus (houses) | husen (the houses) |
| en bok (a book) | böcker (books) | böckerna (the books) |

As a guide, plurals in **-or**, **-ar**, and **-er** usually take **-na**: **flickor → flickorna**, **bilar → bilarna**, and **katter → katterna**. Plurals ending in **-n** often take **-a** (**äpplen → äpplena**), while many ett-word plurals with no ending take **-en** (**hus → husen**). These patterns are not fully predictable, and some nouns also change their vowel, so learn the definite plural with the noun.

With an adjective before the noun, use **de** and the adjective’s definite form: **de röda bilarna** means “the red cars.” The noun still has its definite plural ending.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Böckerna ligger på bordet.** — The books are on the table.
2. **Barnen leker i trädgården.** — The children are playing in the garden.
3. **Bilarna står utanför huset.** — The cars are parked outside the house.
4. **Flickorna går till skolan.** — The girls are walking to school.
5. **Äpplena är mogna.** — The apples are ripe.
6. **Husen vid sjön är röda.** — The houses by the lake are red.
7. **Var är nycklarna?** — Where are the keys?
8. **Tågen går var tionde minut.** — The trains run every ten minutes.
9. **De gamla träden ger mycket skugga.** — The old trees provide a lot of shade.
10. **Jag har tvättat kläderna.** — I have washed the clothes.
