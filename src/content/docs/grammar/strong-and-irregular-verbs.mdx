Many common Swedish verbs do not make their past forms with the regular endings **-ade**, **-de**, or **-te**. Some change the vowel in the verb stem; others have forms that must simply be learned. The same verb form is used with every subject: **jag**, **du**, **hon**, and **vi** do not change the verb.

## Four useful forms

When learning a verb, it is useful to learn these forms together:

| Form | What it is used for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Infinitive | The dictionary form, often used after a modal verb or **att** | **skriva** (to write) |
| Present | An action or situation in the present | **skriver** (write/writes) |
| Preterite | An event or situation in a finished past time | **skrev** (wrote) |
| Supine | Used after **har** to make the present perfect | **skrivit** (written, with *have*) |

For example:

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Jag skriver ett mejl.** | I am writing an email. |
| **Jag skrev ett mejl i går.** | I wrote an email yesterday. |
| **Jag har skrivit ett mejl.** | I have written an email. |

The **supine** is a verb form used after **har**. Do not use the preterite after **har**: say **har skrivit**, not *har skrev*.

## Strong verbs: a vowel changes

A **strong verb** often changes its stem vowel in the preterite and/or supine. It does not use the usual past endings **-ade**, **-de**, or **-te**. Several verbs share similar vowel patterns, but the patterns do not reliably tell you every form. Learn all four forms as a set.

| Infinitive | Present | Preterite | Supine | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **bita** | **biter** | **bet** | **bitit** | bite |
| **bli** | **blir** | **blev** | **blivit** | become |
| **bryta** | **bryter** | **bröt** | **brutit** | break |
| **dricka** | **dricker** | **drack** | **druckit** | drink |
| **falla** | **faller** | **föll** | **fallit** | fall |
| **finna** | **finner** | **fann** | **funnit** | find |
| **flyga** | **flyger** | **flög** | **flugit** | fly |
| **frysa** | **fryser** | **frös** | **frusit** | freeze |
| **hålla** | **håller** | **höll** | **hållit** | hold, keep |
| **ligga** | **ligger** | **låg** | **legat** | lie, be situated |
| **sitta** | **sitter** | **satt** | **suttit** | sit |
| **sjunga** | **sjunger** | **sjöng** | **sjungit** | sing |
| **skriva** | **skriver** | **skrev** | **skrivit** | write |
| **slå** | **slår** | **slog** | **slagit** | hit, strike |
| **springa** | **springer** | **sprang** | **sprungit** | run |
| **stå** | **står** | **stod** | **stått** | stand |
| **äta** | **äter** | **åt** | **ätit** | eat |

Some everyday examples:

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Barnen drack mjölk till frukost.** | The children drank milk with breakfast. |
| **Vi har skrivit ett brev.** | We have written a letter. |
| **Hon sprang till bussen.** | She ran to the bus. |
| **De har suttit här länge.** | They have been sitting here for a long time. |

## Irregular forms that do not follow one pattern

Some frequent verbs have especially unpredictable forms. A few change the stem in both the present and past, and some have forms that cannot be worked out from a general vowel pattern.

| Infinitive | Present | Preterite | Supine | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **vara** | **är** | **var** | **varit** | be |
| **ha** | **har** | **hade** | **haft** | have |
| **gå** | **går** | **gick** | **gått** | go, walk |
| **göra** | **gör** | **gjorde** | **gjort** | do, make |
| **komma** | **kommer** | **kom** | **kommit** | come |
| **få** | **får** | **fick** | **fått** | get, receive; be allowed to |
| **ge** | **ger** | **gav** | **gett** | give |
| **ta** | **tar** | **tog** | **tagit** | take |
| **se** | **ser** | **såg** | **sett** | see |
| **säga** | **säger** | **sa** (also **sade**) | **sagt** | say |
| **veta** | **vet** | **visste** | **vetat** | know (a fact) |
| **välja** | **väljer** | **valde** | **valt** | choose |

The forms of **säga** vary slightly: **sa** is common in speech and writing, while **sade** is also correct. The supine is **sagt**.

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Jag var hemma i går.** | I was at home yesterday. |
| **Vi har redan gjort det.** | We have already done it. |
| **Han fick en present.** | He got a present. |
| **Hon har valt en bok.** | She has chosen a book. |
| **De sa tack och gick hem.** | They said thank you and went home. |

## Preterite and supine have different jobs

Use the **preterite** when you place something in a finished past time. Use **har** with the **supine** when speaking about a past event without placing it in a finished time, or when it matters now. The choice depends on how you present the event, not just on the English translation.

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Vi åt klockan sex.** | We ate at six. |
| **Vi har ätit.** | We have eaten. |
| **Hon gick hem efter jobbet.** | She went home after work. |
| **Hon har gått hem.** | She has gone home. |

Do not confuse the supine with the **-en/-et** form used before a noun or after **är** to describe a state. For example, **har skrivit** uses the supine, but **brevet är skrivet** uses an adjective-like participle: *the letter is written*. At this level, focus on using the supine after **har**.

## Questions and negatives

Irregular verbs follow the same basic sentence patterns as other verbs. In a question, put the verb before the subject. In a main-clause negative sentence, put **inte** after the verb.

| Swedish | English |
|---|---|
| **Såg du filmen i går?** | Did you see the film yesterday? |
| **Jag såg inte filmen.** | I did not see the film. |
| **Har ni ätit?** | Have you eaten? |
| **Vi har inte ätit än.** | We have not eaten yet. |

In a subordinate clause, **inte** usually comes before the verb:

- **Hon stannade hemma eftersom hon inte mådde bra.** (She stayed home because she did not feel well.)

## How to learn them

There is no single rule that predicts all strong and irregular forms. When you learn a new verb, write its infinitive, present, preterite, and supine together. Pay special attention to the preterite and supine, since they often differ from each other as well as from the infinitive.

| Infinitive | Present | Preterite | Supine |
|---|---|---|---|
| **dricka** (drink) | **dricker** | **drack** | **druckit** |
| **gå** (go, walk) | **går** | **gick** | **gått** |
| **skriva** (write) | **skriver** | **skrev** | **skrivit** |
