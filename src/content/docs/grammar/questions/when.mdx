## Using *när* for “when”

Swedish **när** means **when**. It can ask about the time of an event, or introduce a clause that says when an event happens.

### Asking when

In a direct question, **när** comes first, followed by the verb and then the subject:

> **När + verb + subject?**

- **När börjar filmen?** — When does the film start?
- **När kommer du?** — When are you coming?

Swedish does not add an equivalent of English *do*: **När börjar filmen?** is literally “When starts the film?”

In an embedded question, use subject before the verb:

- **Vet du när filmen börjar?** — Do you know when the film starts?
- **Jag minns inte när hon kommer.** — I don’t remember when she is coming.

Compare **När börjar filmen?** (direct question: verb before subject) with **Vet du när filmen börjar?** (embedded question: subject before verb).

### Saying when something happens

**När** can also introduce a time clause—a part of a sentence that tells when something happens. In this clause, put the subject before the verb:

- **Ring mig när du kommer hem.** — Call me when you get home.
- **När lektionen slutar, går vi hem.** — When the lesson ends, we go home.

If the time clause comes first, the main clause has the verb before its subject: **När lektionen slutar, går vi hem** (not *När lektionen slutar, vi går hem*). If the main clause comes first, its usual word order stays the same: **Vi går hem när lektionen slutar.**

Use **när** when you mean that something happens at the time another event occurs. **Om** means “if” when the event is uncertain: **När du har tid, ring mig** means “When you have time, call me”; **Om du har tid, ring mig** means “If you have time, call me.”

## Useful Expressions

1. **När öppnar biblioteket?** — When does the library open?
2. **När går nästa buss?** — When does the next bus leave?
3. **Vet du när tåget kommer?** — Do you know when the train arrives?
4. **Jag vet inte när mötet börjar.** — I don’t know when the meeting starts.
5. **Säg till när du är framme.** — Let me know when you’ve arrived.
6. **Vi äter när alla har kommit.** — We’ll eat when everyone has arrived.
7. **När det regnar, stannar jag hemma.** — When it rains, I stay at home.
8. **Ring mig när du har en minut.** — Call me when you have a minute.
9. **När slutar du jobbet i dag?** — When do you finish work today?
10. **Jag brukar läsa när jag åker tåg.** — I usually read when I travel by train.
