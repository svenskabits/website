---
title: Strong and Irregular Verbs
description: Learn how Swedish strong and irregular verbs form the present, preterite, and supine.
---

Swedish verbs usually follow one of four regular verb groups. **Strong verbs** commonly change their stem vowel in the past forms instead of using the regular group endings. **Irregular verbs** are verbs whose forms do not follow those regular group patterns. Strong verbs are one important kind of irregular verb.

## Learn the four main forms

It is useful to learn these forms together: the infinitive, present, preterite, and supine. The preterite describes a completed past event and can stand alone. The supine is used after **har** to form the perfect.

| Infinitive | Present | Preterite | Supine |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| skriva (to write) | skriver | skrev | skrivit |
| dricka (to drink) | dricker | drack | druckit |
| gå (to go, walk) | går | gick | gått |
| se (to see) | ser | såg | sett |

The vowel often changes between forms, but there is no single pattern that predicts every strong or irregular verb. Learn each verb's forms as a set. The present form is also not always identical to the infinitive.

## In sentences

- Jag **skrev** ett mejl i går. — I wrote an email yesterday.
- Vi **drack** kaffe efter maten. — We drank coffee after the meal.
- Hon **gick** hem tidigt. — She went home early.
- De **såg** en film i helgen. — They saw a film at the weekend.
- Jag **har skrivit** till min vän. — I have written to my friend.
- Vi **har druckit** allt vatten. — We have drunk all the water.
- Han **har gått** till jobbet. — He has gone to work.
- Har du **sett** den här filmen? — Have you seen this film?

In the first four examples, the preterite stands on its own. In the last four, **har** is followed by the supine: **skrivit**, **druckit**, **gått**, or **sett**.

## Common confusion

Do not use the preterite after **har**: say **Jag har skrivit**, not *Jag har skrev*. Conversely, when the preterite is used by itself, say **Jag skrev i går**. English speakers may expect one past form to work in both places, but Swedish distinguishes the preterite from the supine.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Jag skriver ett brev.** — I am writing a letter.
2. **Hon skrev till sin syster.** — She wrote to her sister.
3. **Vi har skrivit en lista.** — We have written a list.
4. **Dricker du kaffe?** — Do you drink coffee?
5. **De drack te i går.** — They drank tea yesterday.
6. **Han har druckit vatten.** — He has drunk water.
7. **Jag går till skolan.** — I am going to school.
8. **Vi gick hem tillsammans.** — We went home together.
9. **Hon har gått till affären.** — She has gone to the shop.
10. **Jag såg en fågel.** — I saw a bird.
