---
title: Imperative
description: Learn how Swedish uses imperative verb forms for commands, instructions, invitations, and advice.
---

The **imperative** is the verb form used to tell someone to do something. It can give a command or instruction, offer an invitation, or give advice. Swedish usually leaves out the subject pronoun **du** (“you”): the verb itself makes clear that you are speaking to someone.

## Forming the imperative

There is not one ending that works for every Swedish verb. Many **group 1** verbs use the **-a** form:

- **tala** (“speak”) → **Tala långsamt!** — Speak slowly!
- **vänta** (“wait”) → **Vänta här.** — Wait here.

Some other verbs use a shorter stem:

- **köpa** (“buy”) → **Köp mjölk!** — Buy milk!
- **sätta** (“put, set”) → **Sätt koppen här.** — Put the cup here.

Common forms to learn include **gå** (“go”), **kom** from **komma** (“come”), **var** from **vara** (“be”), and **gör** from **göra** (“do, make”):

- **Gå hem nu.** — Go home now.
- **Kom hit!** — Come here!
- **Var försiktig.** — Be careful.
- **Gör så här.** — Do it this way.

## Using the imperative

The imperative normally comes first, and the subject **du** is usually not said. It can be used for directions, practical instructions, invitations, and advice:

- **Sväng till vänster.** — Turn left.
- **Ta en kopp kaffe!** — Have a cup of coffee!
- **Ta det lugnt.** — Take it easy.

To make a negative command, put **inte** after the imperative: **Öppna inte dörren.** (“Don’t open the door.”)

## Common confusion: the subject and tone

Do not normally add **du** before a command: say **Kom hit!**, not **Du kom hit!** The latter has statement-like word order and means “You came here.” A bare imperative can sound direct, depending on the situation and tone. A question such as **Kan du öppna fönstret?** (“Can you open the window?”) is another option.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Öppna fönstret.** — Open the window.
2. **Stäng dörren.** — Close the door.
3. **Sätt dig här.** — Sit here.
4. **Läs instruktionen.** — Read the instruction.
5. **Säg till om du behöver hjälp.** — Let me know if you need help.
6. **Gå rakt fram.** — Go straight ahead.
7. **Kom in!** — Come in!
8. **Var tyst en stund.** — Be quiet for a moment.
9. **Gör ett försök till.** — Try one more time.
10. **Låt oss börja!** — Let’s begin!
