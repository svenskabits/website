---
title: Reflexive Verbs
description: Learn how Swedish reflexive verbs use an object pronoun that refers back to the subject.
---

A Swedish **reflexive verb** has an object pronoun that refers back to the subject: the subject does something to or for itself. For example, **tvätta sig** means “wash oneself.” The pronoun changes with the subject:

| Subject | Example | Translation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| jag | *Jag tvättar mig.* | I wash myself. |
| du | *Du tvättar dig.* | You wash yourself. |
| han/hon | *Han/hon tvättar sig.* | He/she washes himself/herself. |
| vi | *Vi tvättar oss.* | We wash ourselves. |
| ni | *Ni tvättar er.* | You wash yourselves. |
| de | *De tvättar sig.* | They wash themselves. |

The verb stays the same across these subjects; it is the reflexive pronoun that changes. In a normal main clause, it follows the verb, as in *Jag tvättar mig*. Many common Swedish verb phrases are learned with their reflexive pronoun:

- **känna sig** — feel: *Jag känner mig trött.* — I feel tired.
- **sätta sig** — sit down: *Vi sätter oss här.* — We sit down here.
- **lägga sig** — lie down, go to bed: *Hon lägger sig tidigt.* — She goes to bed early.
- **skynda sig** — hurry: *De skyndar sig hem.* — They hurry home.
- **klä på sig** — get dressed: *Han klär på sig.* — He gets dressed.

## A useful distinction

Some verbs have a different meaning when used reflexively. **Lägga sig** means “lie down” or “go to bed,” while **lägga boken på bordet** means “put the book on the table.” The object pronoun in the reflexive phrase points back to the subject; *boken* in the other phrase is a separate object.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Jag tvättar mig på morgonen.** — I wash myself in the morning.
2. **Känner du dig bättre?** — Do you feel better?
3. **Vi sätter oss vid fönstret.** — We sit down by the window.
4. **Barnet lägger sig klockan åtta.** — The child goes to bed at eight.
5. **Skynda dig!** — Hurry up!
6. **De skyndar sig till bussen.** — They hurry to the bus.
7. **Jag klär på mig snabbt.** — I get dressed quickly.
8. **Hon tvättar sig efter träningen.** — She washes herself after exercising.
9. **Sätt er här, tack.** — Sit down here, please.
10. **Vi känner oss hemma här.** — We feel at home here.
