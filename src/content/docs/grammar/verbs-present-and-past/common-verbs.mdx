description: Learn the forms and everyday uses of the Swedish verbs vara, ha, göra, and bli.

The verbs **vara** (be), **ha** (have), **göra** (do or make), and **bli** (become or get) are among the most useful Swedish verbs. Their forms are irregular, so learn each set together.

## Four main forms

| Infinitive | Present | Preterite | Supine |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| vara (to be) | är | var | varit |
| ha (to have) | har | hade | haft |
| göra (to do, make) | gör | gjorde | gjort |
| bli (to become, get) | blir | blev | blivit |

The **infinitive** is the basic form, the **present** describes the present, and the **preterite** is used for a completed past event. The **supine** form follows **har** or **hade** in compound past forms: *har varit*, *hade haft*. The subject does not change the verb form: *jag är*, *du är*, *de är*.

## Meanings and examples

- **Vara** describes who or what someone is, or a state or location: *Jag är hemma.* — I am at home.
- **Ha** means to have, possess, or experience: *Vi har en liten lägenhet.* — We have a small apartment.
- **Göra** means to do or make: *Vad gör du?* — What are you doing?
- **Bli** describes a change or result, often translated as “become” or “get”: *Det blir mörkt.* — It is getting dark.

Here are the past and supine forms in context:

- *Hon var sjuk i går.* — She was ill yesterday.
- *Jag hade mycket att göra.* — I had a lot to do.
- *De gjorde en lista tillsammans.* — They made a list together.
- *Vädret blev bättre.* — The weather got better.
- *Han har blivit pappa.* — He has become a father.

## Common confusion: vara or bli?

Use **vara** for a state; use **bli** when that state begins or changes. *Hon är trött* means “She is tired,” while *Hon blir trött* means “She is getting tired.” So **bli** is not simply another word for **vara**.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Jag är redo.** — I am ready.
2. **Vi var på jobbet.** — We were at work.
3. **Har du tid?** — Do you have time?
4. **De hade en hund.** — They had a dog.
5. **Vad gör ni?** — What are you doing?
6. **Jag gjorde mitt bästa.** — I did my best.
7. **Det blir varmt i dag.** — It is getting warm today.
8. **Hon blev glad.** — She became happy.
9. **Vi har varit här förut.** — We have been here before.
10. **Han har gjort kaffe.** — He has made coffee.
