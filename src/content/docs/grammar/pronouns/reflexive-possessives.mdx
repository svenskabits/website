---
title: Reflexive Possessives
description: Learn how sin, sitt, and sina show that something belongs to the subject of the same clause.
---

Swedish uses **sin**, **sitt**, and **sina** to say that something belongs to the subject of the same clause. They are mainly used with third-person subjects such as **han** (he), **hon** (she), **de** (they), or a person's name.

## The forms

Choose the form according to the noun that follows, not the owner:

| Noun | Reflexive possessive | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Singular *en*-word | **sin** | *sin bok* — his, her, or their own book |
| Singular *ett*-word | **sitt** | *sitt hus* — his, her, or their own house |
| Plural noun | **sina** | *sina böcker* — his, her, or their own books |

The form agrees with the thing owned, not the owner's gender. So a man and a woman both say *sin bil* (“their own car”), because *bil* is an *en*-word.

## Examples

- *Anna tog sin bok.* — Anna took her own book.
- *Anna tog hennes bok.* — Anna took another woman's book.
- *Erik sålde sitt hus.* — Erik sold his own house.
- *Barnen hämtade sina väskor.* — The children picked up their own bags.
- *De träffade sin lärare efter lektionen.* — They met their own teacher after the lesson.

## Common confusion

Use **sin**, **sitt**, or **sina** when the owner is the subject of that same clause. Use **hans** (his), **hennes** (her), or **deras** (their) when the owner is someone else, or otherwise is not the subject. Compare *Anna tog sin bok* (“Anna took her own book”) with *Anna tog hennes bok* (“Anna took another woman's book”). The choice depends on who owns the book, not on whether Anna is a woman.

## Useful Expressions

1. *Han läser sin tidning.* — He is reading his own newspaper.
2. *Hon ringde sin mamma.* — She called her own mother.
3. *De städade sitt rum.* — They cleaned their own room.
4. *Sara glömde sina nycklar.* — Sara forgot her own keys.
5. *Johan lagar sin cykel.* — Johan is repairing his own bicycle.
6. *Hon tog sitt paraply.* — She took her own umbrella.
7. *Pojkarna packade sina väskor.* — The boys packed their own bags.
8. *Han tvättar sin bil.* — He is washing his own car.
9. *Lisa gjorde sitt arbete.* — Lisa did her own work.
10. *De besökte sina vänner.* — They visited their own friends.
