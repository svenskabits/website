---
title: No One and None
description: Learn how ingen, inget, and inga mean no, not any, no one, nothing, or none.
---

Swedish **ingen**, **inget**, and **inga** express “no” or “not any” before a noun. Used on their own, they can mean “no one,” “nothing,” or “none.” Choose the form to match the Swedish noun's gender and number.

## Choose the form

| Form | Before a noun | Used on its own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ingen** | Singular *en*-word: *ingen bil* | No one / none: *Ingen kom.* |
| **inget** | Singular *ett*-word: *inget kaffe* | Nothing / none: *Inget hände.* |
| **inga** | Plural noun: *inga frågor* | None: *Inga är lediga.* |

Before a noun, the form comes directly before it:

- **Jag har ingen bil.** — I don't have a car.
- **Det finns inget kaffe.** — There is no coffee.
- **Vi har inga frågor.** — We have no questions.

On their own, the forms refer to a person, thing, or group understood from context:

- **Ingen svarade.** — No one answered.
- **Inget är omöjligt.** — Nothing is impossible.
- **Inga kom i tid.** — None arrived on time.

## Common confusion

Match the form to the Swedish noun, not to the English word that follows “no”: **ingen stol** (*en*), **inget bord** (*ett*), but **inga stolar** (plural). The related forms **någon**, **något**, and **några** mean “someone/something/some” or, before a noun, “any/some”; use **ingen**, **inget**, and **inga** for the corresponding “no” meanings.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Jag har ingen tid.** — I don't have any time.
2. **Hon har ingen syster.** — She has no sister.
3. **Det finns ingen buss nu.** — There is no bus now.
4. **Vi har inget socker.** — We have no sugar.
5. **Jag hör inget ljud.** — I hear no sound.
6. **Det är inget problem.** — It's no problem.
7. **De har inga barn.** — They have no children.
8. **Det finns inga biljetter kvar.** — There are no tickets left.
9. **Ingen vet svaret.** — No one knows the answer.
10. **Inget förändrades.** — Nothing changed.
