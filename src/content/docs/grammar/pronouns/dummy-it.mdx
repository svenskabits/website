---
title: Dummy It
description: Learn how Swedish det fills the subject position in weather, time, and evaluative sentences without referring to a specific thing.
---

Swedish **det** can be a grammatical, or “dummy,” subject. It fills the subject position in a sentence but does not refer to a particular person or thing. English often uses **it** in the same way.

## Use *det* as a dummy subject

Use **det** in common statements about weather, time, and general conditions:

- **Det regnar.** — It is raining.
- **Det är sent.** — It is late.
- **Det är varmt här.** — It is warm here.

It also appears in evaluative patterns such as **Det är + adjective + att + infinitive**. The adjective gives an evaluation, and the *att*-phrase says what is being evaluated:

- **Det är svårt att läsa den här texten.** — It is difficult to read this text.
- **Det är roligt att lära sig svenska.** — It is fun to learn Swedish.

In these statements, **det** comes before the finite verb, as the subject normally does. The sentence is about the weather, time, or a situation—not about an object called “it.”

The related **Det finns** (“there is/are”) pattern also uses **det** as a dummy subject; it is covered separately under Common Structures.

## Common confusion

Not every **det** means dummy “it.” When it refers back to an *ett*-word noun, **det** means “it” and stands for that thing: **Huset är stort. Det ligger vid sjön.** — “The house is big. It is by the lake.” Here **det** refers to **huset**; in **Det regnar**, it refers to nothing specific.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Det regnar ute.** — It is raining outside.
2. **Det snöar fortfarande.** — It is still snowing.
3. **Det blåser mycket idag.** — It is very windy today.
4. **Det är kallt i köket.** — It is cold in the kitchen.
5. **Det är fredag idag.** — It is Friday today.
6. **Det är redan kväll.** — It is already evening.
7. **Det är långt till stationen.** — It is a long way to the station.
8. **Det är lätt att förstå.** — It is easy to understand.
9. **Det är svårt att hitta parkering här.** — It is hard to find parking here.
10. **Det känns bra att vara hemma.** — It feels good to be home.
