---
title: Some and Someone
description: Learn when to use någon, något, and några for someone, something, some, or any.
---

Swedish uses **någon**, **något**, and **några** for meanings such as “someone,” “something,” “some,” and “any.” Choose the form according to the Swedish noun—not the English translation.

## Choose the form

| Form | Use before a noun | Use on its own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **någon** | Singular *en*-word: *någon fråga* | Someone / anyone: *Någon ringde.* |
| **något** | Singular *ett*-word: *något äpple* | Something / anything: *Något hände.* |
| **några** | Plural noun: *några frågor* | Some / a few: *Några väntar.* |

Before a noun, these words come directly before its indefinite form:

- **Har du någon fråga?** — Do you have any questions?
- **Har du något äpple?** — Do you have an apple? / Do you have any apples?
- **Vi har några gäster.** — We have some guests.

On their own, **någon** means “someone” or “anyone,” **något** means “something” or “anything,” and **några** means “some” or “a few”:

- **Någon väntar utanför.** — Someone is waiting outside.
- **Vill du säga något?** — Do you want to say something?
- **Några har redan gått.** — A few have already left.

The best English translation depends on the sentence. For example, **någon** means “someone” when it refers to a person, but can mean “any” in a question: **Någon ringde.** (“Someone called.”) and **Har du någon fråga?** (“Do you have any questions?”).

## Common confusion

Do not choose among **någon**, **något**, and **några** based on whether English uses “someone,” “something,” “some,” or “any.” Check the Swedish noun: **någon fråga** (*en*), **något äpple** (*ett*), **några frågor** (plural). In negative sentences, these forms are usually replaced by **ingen**, **inget**, or **inga**; those forms are covered in a separate lesson.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Någon ringer.** — Someone is calling.
2. **Har du någon penna?** — Do you have a pen?
3. **Finns det någon ledig plats?** — Is there any seat available?
4. **Har du något papper?** — Do you have any paper?
5. **Något är fel.** — Something is wrong.
6. **Vill du ha något att dricka?** — Would you like something to drink?
7. **Några barn leker på gården.** — Some children are playing in the yard.
8. **Jag har några frågor.** — I have a few questions.
9. **Några väntar redan.** — A few are already waiting.
10. **Vi behöver några minuter.** — We need a few minutes.
