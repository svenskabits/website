## Swedish noun plurals

Swedish nouns change form when you talk about more than one. The plural form is used after numbers and words such as **många** (“many”). Swedish has no separate plural indefinite article like English *some*:

- **en bok** — a book; **två böcker** — two books
- **ett äpple** — an apple; **många äpplen** — many apples

Plural endings depend on the noun, so it is useful to learn a noun together with its plural. These are common patterns:

| Singular | Indefinite plural | Definite plural | Common pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| en flicka (a girl) | flickor (girls) | flickorna (the girls) | **-a** often changes to **-or** |
| en bil (a car) | bilar (cars) | bilarna (the cars) | many en-nouns take **-ar** |
| en katt (a cat) | katter (cats) | katterna (the cats) | many en-nouns take **-er** |
| ett äpple (an apple) | äpplen (apples) | äpplena (the apples) | some ett-nouns take **-n** |
| ett hus (a house) | hus (houses) | husen (the houses) | some nouns have no plural ending |

These are guides, not rules that predict every noun. Some plurals also change the vowel or use a less predictable form: **en bok – böcker** (a book – books), **en fot – fötter** (a foot – feet), and **ett barn – barn** (a child – children).

### Indefinite and definite plural

Use the indefinite plural when you mean plural things in general or introduce them: **Jag köper äpplen** — “I’m buying apples.” To mean **the** plural things, use the definite plural form: **Äpplena är röda** — “The apples are red.” The ending varies with the noun, as the table shows.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Jag har två systrar.** — I have two sisters.
2. **Barnen leker ute.** — The children are playing outside.
3. **Böckerna ligger på bordet.** — The books are on the table.
4. **Vi köper äpplen.** — We’re buying apples.
5. **De gamla husen ligger nära sjön.** — The old houses are near the lake.
6. **Hundarna behöver vatten.** — The dogs need water.
7. **Mina vänner bor i Göteborg.** — My friends live in Gothenburg.
8. **Det finns många stolar i rummet.** — There are many chairs in the room.
9. **Var är nycklarna?** — Where are the keys?
10. **Tågen går varje timme.** — The trains run every hour.
