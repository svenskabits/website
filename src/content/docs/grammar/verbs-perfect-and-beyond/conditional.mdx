---
title: The Conditional with “Would”
description: Learn how Swedish uses skulle with an infinitive to express hypothetical results, past intentions, and polite requests.
---

Swedish commonly uses **skulle + infinitive** to express “would.” The infinitive that follows **skulle** normally appears **without att**.

## The basic pattern

Use **skulle** before the infinitive. It has the same form with every subject:

> **subject + skulle + infinitive**

- **Jag skulle stanna hemma.** — I would stay at home.
- **De skulle köpa ett större hus.** — They would buy a bigger house.
- **Vi skulle gärna hjälpa till.** — We would be happy to help.

With a condition introduced by **om** (“if”), **skulle** can describe a hypothetical result:

- **Om jag hade tid, skulle jag resa mer.** — If I had time, I would travel more.

When the **om** clause comes first, the verb **skulle** comes before the subject in the following main clause: **skulle jag**, not *jag skulle*.

## Other common uses

**Skulle** can also describe an intention from a past point of view, often translated as “would” or “was going to”:

- **Hon sa att hon skulle ringa senare.** — She said that she would call later.

It is also common in polite requests, offers, and suggestions:

- **Skulle du kunna hjälpa mig?** — Could you help me?
- **Skulle du vilja ha lite kaffe?** — Would you like some coffee?

## Common confusion: *skulle* and *ska*

**Ska** usually presents something as a plan or intention now, or as something that will happen. **Skulle** commonly means “would,” or reports a plan from a past viewpoint:

- **Jag ska ringa henne.** — I’m going to call her.
- **Jag sa att jag skulle ringa henne.** — I said that I would call her.

After either modal verb, use the infinitive without **att**: **ska ringa**, **skulle ringa**.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Om jag vann, skulle jag köpa ett hus.** — If I won, I would buy a house.
2. **Vi skulle gärna stanna längre.** — We would be happy to stay longer.
3. **Skulle du kunna öppna fönstret?** — Could you open the window?
4. **Skulle du vilja följa med?** — Would you like to come along?
5. **De skulle ta tåget om det gick.** — They would take the train if it were running.
6. **Han sa att han skulle komma snart.** — He said that he would come soon.
7. **Jag skulle välja den blå tröjan.** — I would choose the blue sweater.
8. **Skulle vi kunna börja nu?** — Could we start now?
9. **Hon trodde att det skulle regna.** — She thought it would rain.
10. **Vad skulle du göra?** — What would you do?
