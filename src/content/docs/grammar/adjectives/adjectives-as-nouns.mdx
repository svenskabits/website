## The noun can be understood from context

In Swedish, an adjective can stand without an expressed noun when listeners already know which people or things it refers to. The adjective describes an implied noun, and the context tells you what that noun is. This is often called a **substantivized adjective**.

These phrases commonly have **den**, **det**, or **de** before the adjective. The adjective takes a form that fits the implied noun:

| Implied noun | Phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **människor** (people, plural) | **de unga** | the young (people) |
| **personer** (people, plural) | **de rika** | the rich (people) |
| **en person** (a person, singular) | **den sjuka** | the sick person |
| **ett alternativ** (an option, singular) | **det bästa** | the best (option/thing) |

For example, if a conversation is about several possible routes, **det bästa** can mean “the best option.” If it is about several people, **de unga** can mean “the young people.” The noun is not missing at random: the listener recovers it from what is being discussed. The adjective still has an appropriate form, such as plural **unga** in **de unga** or neuter singular **bästa** in **det bästa**.

More examples:

- **De rika har ofta fler valmöjligheter.** — The rich often have more choices.
- **Den sjuka behöver vila.** — The sick person needs to rest.
- **Det bästa är att vänta.** — The best thing is to wait.

## Common confusion

Do not treat the adjective as if it simply becomes a noun with one unchanging form. The article and adjective form matter: say **de unga**, not *de ung*. Also use context to make clear who or what the implied noun refers to.

## Useful Expressions

1. **De unga sitter längst fram.** — The young people are sitting at the front.
2. **De rika har många möjligheter.** — The rich have many opportunities.
3. **De äldre får gå in först.** — Older people may go in first.
4. **De små leker i trädgården.** — The little ones are playing in the garden.
5. **De arbetslösa behöver stöd.** — The unemployed need support.
6. **De sjuka behöver vila.** — Sick people need to rest.
7. **De bästa kommer först.** — The best ones come first.
8. **De ansvariga svarar snart.** — The people responsible will reply soon.
9. **Det bästa är att fråga.** — The best thing is to ask.
10. **Det viktiga är att komma i tid.** — The important thing is to arrive on time.
