description: Learn how Swedish adjectives take a definite form in phrases such as den röda bilen and det röda huset.

## The definite form

When an adjective comes before a definite noun, Swedish commonly uses the adjective’s **-a form**. The phrase also has **den**, **det**, or **de** before the adjective, and the noun has its definite ending:

| Indefinite phrase | Definite phrase |
| --- | --- |
| **en röd bil** — a red car | **den röda bilen** — the red car |
| **ett rött hus** — a red house | **det röda huset** — the red house |
| **röda bilar** — red cars | **de röda bilarna** — the red cars |

Notice that the adjective is **röd** with an en-word and **rött** with an ett-word in the indefinite singular. In the definite phrases, the common adjective form is **röda** for all three: **den röda**, **det röda**, and **de röda**. The noun’s definite ending remains as well: **bilen**, **huset**, **bilarna**.

More examples:

- **Den gamla bron är vacker.** — The old bridge is beautiful.
- **Vi bor i det gula huset.** — We live in the yellow house.
- **De nya böckerna ligger på bordet.** — The new books are on the table.

The **-a** form is the usual pattern, but it is not a rule that every adjective simply adds **-a**. Some adjectives have other forms or variants; for example, **den gamle mannen** is also used for “the old man,” alongside **den gamla mannen**.

## Common confusion

Do not use the adjective’s indefinite form in these phrases: say **den röda bilen**, not *den röd bilen*. Also keep the definite form on the noun, as in **bilen** and **huset**.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Den röda bilen är min.** — The red car is mine.
2. **Det röda huset ligger nära stationen.** — The red house is near the station.
3. **De röda bilarna står här.** — The red cars are parked here.
4. **Jag gillar den nya boken.** — I like the new book.
5. **Var ligger det stora hotellet?** — Where is the large hotel?
6. **De små barnen leker ute.** — The small children are playing outside.
7. **Vi köpte den blå jackan.** — We bought the blue jacket.
8. **Han stängde det öppna fönstret.** — He closed the open window.
9. **Jag såg de gamla husen.** — I saw the old houses.
10. **Hon hjälpte den gamle mannen.** — She helped the old man.
