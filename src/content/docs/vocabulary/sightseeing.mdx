## Places and sights

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en sevärdhet** | a sight, an attraction | Slottet är en populär **sevärdhet**. | The castle is a popular attraction. |
| **ett museum** | a museum | Vi besökte **museet** i går. | We visited the museum yesterday. |
| **ett slott** | a castle | **Slottet** ligger nära centrum. | The castle is near the city centre. |
| **en kyrka** | a church | Den gamla **kyrkan** ligger vid torget. | The old church is by the square. |
| **en staty** | a statue | Många turister fotograferar **statyn**. | Many tourists photograph the statue. |
| **ett torg** | a square | Vi åt lunch på **torget**. | We had lunch in the square. |
| **en utsikt** | a view | Från tornet har man fin **utsikt** över staden. | From the tower, you get a lovely view of the city. |
| **en utställning** | an exhibition | **Utställningen** handlar om svensk historia. | The exhibition is about Swedish history. |
| **Gamla stan** | the old town | Vi promenerade genom **Gamla stan**. | We walked through the old town. |

## Visiting sights

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **besöka** | to visit | Vi ska **besöka** museet efter lunch. | We are going to visit the museum after lunch. |
| **en guidad tur** | a guided tour | Vi följde med på **en guidad tur** på slottet. | We joined a guided tour of the castle. |
| **en guide** | a guide | **Guiden** berättade om stadens historia. | The guide told us about the city's history. |
| **en karta** | a map | Jag tittade på **kartan** innan vi gick. | I looked at the map before we left. |
| **en biljett** | a ticket | Du kan köpa **biljetter** vid entrén. | You can buy tickets at the entrance. |
| **en entréavgift** | an entrance fee | Det finns ingen **entréavgift** på söndagar. | There is no entrance fee on Sundays. |
| **öppettider** | opening hours | Museets **öppettider** finns på dörren. | The museum's opening hours are on the door. |
| **fotografera** | to photograph | Det är tillåtet att **fotografera** här. | You are allowed to take photographs here. |
| **promenera** | to walk, to stroll | Vi **promenerade** längs vattnet efter besöket. | We walked along the water after the visit. |
