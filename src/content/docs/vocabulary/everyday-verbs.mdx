## Daily routines

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **vakna** | to wake up | Jag **vaknar** klockan sju. | I wake up at seven. |
| **gå upp** | to get up | Vi **går upp** tidigt. | We get up early. |
| **duscha** | to shower | Jag **duschar** på morgonen. | I shower in the morning. |
| **äta** | to eat | Vi **äter** middag hemma. | We eat dinner at home. |
| **dricka** | to drink | Hon **dricker** vatten. | She drinks water. |
| **laga mat** | to cook | Pappa **lagar mat** i dag. | Dad is cooking today. |
| **sova** | to sleep | Barnet **sover** nu. | The child is sleeping now. |
| **städa** | to clean | Vi **städar** på lördag. | We clean on Saturday. |

## Getting around and meeting people

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **gå** | to go, to walk | Jag **går** till jobbet. | I walk to work. |
| **åka** | to travel, to ride | Vi **åker** buss till skolan. | We take the bus to school. |
| **komma** | to come | Hon **kommer** hem klockan fem. | She comes home at five. |
| **träffa** | to meet | Jag **träffar** en vän i dag. | I meet a friend today. |
| **ringa** | to call | Jag **ringer** min mamma. | I call my mother. |

## Home and free time

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **bo** | to live | Vi **bor** i Malmö. | We live in Malmö. |
| **jobba** | to work | Han **jobbar** på ett café. | He works at a café. |

## Everyday needs

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **vara** | to be | Jag **är** hemma nu. | I am at home now. |
| **ha** | to have | Vi **har** en katt. | We have a cat. |
| **göra** | to do | Vad **gör** du i dag? | What are you doing today? |
| **köpa** | to buy | Hon **köper** mjölk i affären. | She buys milk at the store. |
| **betala** | to pay | Jag **betalar** med kort. | I pay by card. |
| **vilja** | to want | Jag **vill** ha kaffe. | I want coffee. |
| **behöva** | to need | Vi **behöver** mer tid. | We need more time. |
| **kunna** | to be able to | Han **kan** simma. | He can swim. |
| **tycka om** | to like | Jag **tycker om** te. | I like tea. |
