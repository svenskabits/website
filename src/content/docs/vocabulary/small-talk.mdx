## Conversation

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **småprata** | to make small talk | Vi **småpratade** på bussen. | We made small talk on the bus. |
| **prata** | to talk | De **pratade** en stund efter mötet. | They talked for a while after the meeting. |
| **fråga** | to ask | Han **frågade** hur min dag hade varit. | He asked how my day had been. |
| **berätta** | to tell | Hon **berättade** om sin resa för oss. | She told us about her trip. |
| **träffa** | to meet | Jag **träffade** en granne i affären. | I met a neighbour at the shop. |
| **en granne** | a neighbour | Vår **granne** brukar hälsa på oss. | Our neighbour usually says hello to us. |
| **en kollega** | a colleague | Jag åt lunch med en **kollega**. | I had lunch with a colleague. |

## Everyday topics

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **väder** | weather | **Vädret** blev bättre på eftermiddagen. | The weather got better in the afternoon. |
| **en helg** | a weekend | Vad gjorde du i **helgen**? | What did you do at the weekend? |
| **en semester** | a holiday; a vacation | Vi åker till Malmö på **semestern**. | We are going to Malmö on holiday. |
| **ett jobb** | a job | Hon trivs bra på sitt **jobb**. | She is happy at her job. |
| **en resa** | a trip | De planerar en **resa** till Norge. | They are planning a trip to Norway. |
| **fritid** | free time | På min **fritid** lagar jag gärna mat. | In my free time, I like cooking. |
| **solig** | sunny | Det var **soligt** hela dagen. | It was sunny all day. |
| **varm** | warm | Det blev **varmt** i går. | It got warm yesterday. |
| **kall** | cold | Det är **kallt** ute i dag. | It is cold outside today. |
