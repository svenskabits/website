| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en inbjudan** | an invitation | Jag fick **en inbjudan** till festen. | I got an invitation to the party. |
| **bjuda in** | to invite | Hon **bjöd in** sina grannar på middag. | She invited her neighbours to dinner. |
| **en gäst** | a guest | Varje **gäst** fick ett glas vatten. | Each guest got a glass of water. |
| **en värd** | a host | **Värden** hälsade alla välkomna. | The host welcomed everyone. |
| **tacka ja** | to accept | Jag **tackade ja** till inbjudan. | I accepted the invitation. |
| **tacka nej** | to decline | Han **tackade nej** eftersom han arbetade. | He declined because he was working. |
| **svara** | to reply | Kan du **svara** före fredag? | Can you reply before Friday? |
| **senast** | by, no later than | Svara **senast** på torsdag. | Reply by Thursday at the latest. |
| **komma** | to come | Kan du **komma** på lördag? | Can you come on Saturday? |
| **ta med** | to bring along | Ska jag **ta med** något till festen? | Should I bring anything to the party? |
| **en fest** | a party | Vi ordnar **en fest** på lördag. | We're having a party on Saturday. |
| **ett kalas** | a party, a celebration | Barnen hade **ett kalas** hemma. | The children had a party at home. |
| **en middag** | a dinner | De bjuder på **middag** på fredag. | They're hosting dinner on Friday. |
| **en present** | a gift | Jag köpte **en present** till värden. | I bought a gift for the host. |
| **en adress** | an address | Skicka **adressen** till mig. | Send me the address. |
| **en klädkod** | a dress code | Det finns ingen **klädkod** på festen. | There is no dress code at the party. |
