| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en familj** | a family | Min **familj** bor i Malmö. | My family lives in Malmö. |
| **en mamma** | a mother | Min **mamma** bor här. | My mother lives here. |
| **en pappa** | a father | Min **pappa** lagar mat. | My father cooks. |
| **en förälder** | a parent | En **förälder** hämtar barnet. | A parent picks up the child. |
| **ett barn** | a child | **Barnet** leker hemma. | The child plays at home. |
| **en bror** | a brother | Min **bror** spelar fotboll. | My brother plays football. |
| **en syster** | a sister | Min **syster** läser en bok. | My sister reads a book. |
| **ett syskon** | a sibling | Jag har två **syskon**. | I have two siblings. |
| **en son** | a son | Vår **son** går i skolan. | Our son goes to school. |
| **en dotter** | a daughter | Vår **dotter** är fem år. | Our daughter is five years old. |
| **en man** | a husband | Hennes **man** heter Ali. | Her husband is called Ali. |
| **en fru** | a wife | Hans **fru** arbetar här. | His wife works here. |
| **en partner** | a partner | Min **partner** heter Kim. | My partner is called Kim. |
