## Greetings

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **hej** | hi; hello | **Hej**, jag heter Anna. | Hi, my name is Anna. |
| **hallå** | hello | **Hallå**, är du där? | Hello, are you there? |
| **god morgon** | good morning | **God morgon**, Ali! | Good morning, Ali! |
| **god kväll** | good evening | **God kväll**, välkommen in! | Good evening, come in! |
| **välkommen** | welcome | **Välkommen** hem till oss! | Welcome to our home! |

## Goodbyes

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **hej då** | goodbye; bye | **Hej då**, vi ses i morgon. | Bye, see you tomorrow. |
| **ha det bra** | take care | **Ha det bra** på jobbet! | Take care at work! |
| **vi ses** | see you | **Vi ses** snart igen. | See you again soon. |
| **vi hörs** | talk to you later | **Vi hörs** på telefon. | Talk to you on the phone. |
| **god natt** | good night | **God natt**, mamma! | Good night, Mom! |
