| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ett vatten** | water | Jag dricker **vatten** varje dag. | I drink water every day. |
| **ett kaffe** | coffee | Hon dricker **kaffe** på morgonen. | She drinks coffee in the morning. |
| **ett te** | tea | Han dricker **te** på kvällen. | He drinks tea in the evening. |
| **en mjölk** | milk | Barnet dricker **mjölk** till frukost. | The child drinks milk for breakfast. |
| **en juice** | juice | Jag dricker **juice** till frukost. | I drink juice for breakfast. |
| **en saft** | cordial; squash | Vi dricker **saft** på sommaren. | We drink cordial in the summer. |
| **en läsk** | a soft drink | Hon dricker **läsk** till maten. | She drinks a soft drink with her meal. |
| **en lemonad** | lemonade | De dricker **lemonad** i trädgården. | They drink lemonade in the garden. |
| **ett öl** | a beer | Han beställer **ett öl**. | He orders a beer. |
| **ett vin** | wine | De dricker **vin** till middagen. | They drink wine with dinner. |
