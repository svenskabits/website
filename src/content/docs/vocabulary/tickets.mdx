## Tickets and payment

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en biljett** | a ticket | Jag köpte en **biljett** i appen. | I bought a ticket in the app. |
| **en enkelbiljett** | a single ticket | En **enkelbiljett** gäller för en resa. | A single ticket is valid for one journey. |
| **en periodbiljett** | a period ticket | Hon har en **periodbiljett** för en vecka. | She has a period ticket for one week. |
| **ett månadskort** | a monthly pass | Mitt **månadskort** gäller hela månaden. | My monthly pass is valid for the whole month. |
| **en vuxenbiljett** | an adult ticket | Jag behöver en **vuxenbiljett**. | I need an adult ticket. |
| **en barnbiljett** | a child ticket | Barnet fick en **barnbiljett**. | The child got a child ticket. |
| **en studentbiljett** | a student ticket | Han köpte en **studentbiljett** med rabatt. | He bought a student ticket at a discount. |
| **en biljettmaskin** | a ticket machine | **Biljettmaskinen** tar kort. | The ticket machine accepts cards. |
| **en app** | an app | Du kan köpa biljetten i en **app**. | You can buy the ticket in an app. |
| **ett resekort** | a travel card | Hon laddade sitt **resekort** med pengar. | She topped up her travel card with money. |
| **ett pris** | a price | Vad är **priset** för en enkelbiljett? | What is the price of a single ticket? |
| **en rabatt** | a discount | Studenter får **rabatt** på biljetten. | Students get a discount on the ticket. |

## Using a ticket

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **köpa** | to buy | Vi **köpte** biljetterna på stationen. | We bought the tickets at the station. |
| **betala** | to pay | Jag **betalade** med kort. | I paid by card. |
| **ladda** | to top up; to load | Han **laddade** sitt resekort i appen. | He topped up his travel card in the app. |
| **blippa** | to tap | Du **blippar** kortet vid läsaren. | You tap the card at the reader. |
| **visa** | to show | Hon **visade** sin biljett för kontrollanten. | She showed her ticket to the inspector. |
| **giltig** | valid | Biljetten är **giltig** till klockan tio. | The ticket is valid until ten o'clock. |
| **en biljettkontroll** | a ticket inspection | Det var en **biljettkontroll** på tåget. | There was a ticket inspection on the train. |
| **en kontrollant** | an inspector | **Kontrollanten** bad om våra biljetter. | The inspector asked for our tickets. |
