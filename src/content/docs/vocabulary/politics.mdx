## Elections and government

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ett parti** | a political party | **Partiet** presenterade sitt valprogram i våras. | The party presented its election platform this spring. |
| **en väljare** | a voter | Många **väljare** vill veta hur förslaget ska finansieras. | Many voters want to know how the proposal will be funded. |
| **ett val** | an election | Nästa **val** hålls om fyra år. | The next election will be held in four years. |
| **rösta** | to vote | Hon **röstade** på ett parti som prioriterar klimatet. | She voted for a party that prioritizes the climate. |
| **en valrörelse** | an election campaign | Under **valrörelsen** besökte kandidaterna flera mindre orter. | During the election campaign, the candidates visited several smaller towns. |
| **en riksdag** | a parliament; the Swedish Parliament | **Riksdagen** debatterar och beslutar om nya lagar. | Parliament debates and decides on new laws. |
| **en regering** | a government | **Regeringen** lade fram en ny budget för riksdagen. | The government presented a new budget to parliament. |
| **en opposition** | an opposition | **Oppositionen** kräver tydligare besked från regeringen. | The opposition is demanding a clearer response from the government. |
| **en koalition** | a coalition | Partierna bildade en **koalition** efter valet. | The parties formed a coalition after the election. |
| **en ledamot** | a member of parliament | **Ledamoten** ställde en fråga till ministern. | The member of parliament asked the minister a question. |
| **en mandatperiod** | a term of office | Under **mandatperioden** genomfördes flera stora reformer. | Several major reforms were carried out during the term of office. |
| **en majoritet** | a majority | En **majoritet** av ledamöterna stödde förslaget. | A majority of the members supported the proposal. |

## Debate and policy

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en politisk fråga** | a political issue | Skolan blev en viktig **politisk fråga** inför valet. | Education became an important political issue ahead of the election. |
| **en debatt** | a debate | **Debatten** handlade om hur vården ska finansieras. | The debate was about how healthcare should be funded. |
| **ett förslag** | a proposal | **Förslaget** fick stöd från flera partier. | The proposal received support from several parties. |
| **en motion** | a parliamentary motion | Ledamoten lämnade in en **motion** om bättre kollektivtrafik. | The member of parliament submitted a motion on improving public transport. |
| **en omröstning** | a vote | **Omröstningen** slutade med lika många röster på båda sidor. | The vote ended with the same number of votes on both sides. |
| **en kompromiss** | a compromise | Efter långa förhandlingar nådde partierna en **kompromiss**. | After lengthy negotiations, the parties reached a compromise. |
| **en reform** | a reform | **Reformen** ska göra stödet mer tillgängligt för familjer. | The reform is intended to make support more accessible to families. |
| **ett beslut** | a decision | **Beslutet** väckte kritik från både företag och fackförbund. | The decision drew criticism from both businesses and trade unions. |
| **förhandla** | to negotiate | Partierna **förhandlade** om innehållet i budgeten. | The parties negotiated the contents of the budget. |
| **föreslå** | to propose | Utskottet **föreslog** flera ändringar i lagförslaget. | The committee proposed several changes to the bill. |
| **genomföra** | to implement; to carry out | Kommunen ska **genomföra** reformen stegvis. | The municipality will implement the reform gradually. |
| **prioritera** | to prioritize | Regeringen måste **prioritera** mellan olika samhällsbehov. | The government has to prioritize among different social needs. |
