## Childhood experiences

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ett minne** | a memory | Ett varmt **minne** från barndomen är när vi badade i sjön. | A warm memory from childhood is when we swam in the lake. |
| **en barndomsvän** | a childhood friend | Jag har fortfarande kontakt med en **barndomsvän** från min gamla hemstad. | I am still in touch with a childhood friend from my old hometown. |
| **en leksak** | a toy | Jag sparade min favoritleksak eftersom den påminner mig om min syster. | I kept my favorite toy because it reminds me of my sister. |
| **ett gosedjur** | a soft toy; a stuffed animal | Hon hade alltid med sig sitt **gosedjur** när familjen reste bort. | She always took her stuffed animal with her when the family went away. |
| **en koja** | a den; a hut | Vi byggde en **koja** av filtar och låtsades att vi var på äventyr. | We built a den out of blankets and pretended we were on an adventure. |
| **en gård** | a yard; a courtyard | På **gården** lekte barnen tills det blev mörkt på sommaren. | In the yard, the children played until it got dark in the summer. |
| **en skolavslutning** | an end-of-school celebration | På varje **skolavslutning** sjöng klassen en sång tillsammans. | At every end-of-school celebration, the class sang a song together. |
| **ett sommarlov** | a summer holiday | Under **sommarlovet** brukade vi hälsa på våra kusiner på landet. | During the summer holiday, we used to visit our cousins in the countryside. |
| **en utflykt** | an outing | Jag minns en **utflykt** då vi gick vilse i skogen men hittade tillbaka. | I remember an outing when we got lost in the forest but found our way back. |
| **ett hyss** | a prank | Mitt största **hyss** var att gömma pappas skor före en fest. | My biggest prank was hiding Dad's shoes before a party. |

## Remembering and telling stories

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **minnas** | to remember | Jag **minns** hur stolt jag var när jag lärde mig cykla. | I remember how proud I was when I learned to ride a bike. |
| **komma ihåg** | to remember | Hon **kommer ihåg** namnet på nästan alla sina lärare. | She remembers the names of almost all her teachers. |
| **glömma** | to forget | Jag har **glömt** vad vår första hund hette. | I have forgotten what our first dog was called. |
| **sakna** | to miss | Ibland **saknar** han tiden då hela familjen bodde tillsammans. | Sometimes he misses the time when the whole family lived together. |
| **känna igen** | to recognize | Jag **kände igen** huset på fotografiet från min barndom. | I recognized the house in the photograph from my childhood. |
| **påminna om** | to remind someone of | Den gamla melodin **påminner om** somrarna hos mormor. | The old melody reminds me of the summers at Grandma's. |
| **berätta** | to tell; to recount | Min farfar **berättade** ofta historier om sitt första jobb. | My grandfather often told stories about his first job. |
| **en berättelse** | a story | Hennes **berättelse** om skolresan får oss alltid att skratta. | Her story about the school trip always makes us laugh. |
| **ett fotografi** | a photograph | Vi hittade ett **fotografi** från familjens första semester tillsammans. | We found a photograph from the family's first holiday together. |

## Talking about the past

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **förr** | in the past; before | **Förr** cyklade jag till skolan varje morgon, även när det regnade. | In the past, I cycled to school every morning, even when it was raining. |
| **på den tiden** | at that time; back then | **På den tiden** fanns det ingen dator hemma hos oss. | Back then, we did not have a computer at home. |
| **som barn** | as a child | **Som barn** tillbringade hon många helger hos sina morföräldrar. | As a child, she spent many weekends at her grandparents' house. |
| **bruka** | to be in the habit of | Vi **brukade** plocka blåbär tillsammans innan skolan började igen. | We used to pick blueberries together before school started again. |
| **en gång** | once | **En gång** råkade jag låsa in mig i förrådet under en lek. | Once, I accidentally locked myself in the storage room during a game. |
