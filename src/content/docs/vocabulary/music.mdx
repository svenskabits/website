## Music and styles

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **musik** | music | Jag lyssnar på **musik** hemma. | I listen to music at home. |
| **pop** | pop music | Min syster gillar **pop**. | My sister likes pop music. |
| **rock** | rock music | Vi lyssnar på **rock** hemma. | We listen to rock at home. |
| **jazz** | jazz | Pappa lyssnar på **jazz**. | Dad listens to jazz. |
| **klassisk musik** | classical music | Hon lyssnar på **klassisk musik**. | She listens to classical music. |

## Music words

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en låt** | a song | Jag gillar **låten**. | I like the song. |
| **en sång** | a song | Barnen sjunger en **sång**. | The children sing a song. |
| **en artist** | an artist | **Artisten** sjunger på scenen. | The artist sings on stage. |
| **ett band** | a band | **Bandet** spelar i kväll. | The band plays tonight. |
| **en musiker** | a musician | **Musikern** spelar piano. | The musician plays the piano. |
| **ett instrument** | an instrument | Jag spelar **ett instrument**. | I play an instrument. |
| **en gitarr** | a guitar | Hon spelar **gitarr** hemma. | She plays guitar at home. |
| **ett piano** | a piano | Han spelar **piano** varje dag. | He plays piano every day. |
| **en konsert** | a concert | Vi går på **en konsert** i kväll. | We are going to a concert tonight. |
| **en biljett** | a ticket | Jag köper **en biljett** till konserten. | I buy a ticket to the concert. |
| **en spellista** | a playlist | Jag lyssnar på **spellistan** hemma. | I listen to the playlist at home. |
