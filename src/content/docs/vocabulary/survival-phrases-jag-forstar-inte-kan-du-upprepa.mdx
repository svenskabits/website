## Communication verbs

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **förstå** | to understand | Jag **förstår** frågan. | I understand the question. |
| **upprepa** | to repeat | Läraren **upprepar** ordet. | The teacher repeats the word. |
| **prata** | to speak | Vi **pratar** svenska i klassen. | We speak Swedish in class. |
| **säga** | to say | Hon **säger** ordet tydligt. | She says the word clearly. |
| **fråga** | to ask | Eleven **frågar** läraren. | The student asks the teacher. |
| **svara** | to answer | Eleven **svarar** på frågan. | The student answers the question. |
| **hjälpa** | to help | Läraren **hjälper** eleven. | The teacher helps the student. |
| **behöva** | to need | Jag **behöver** hjälp med ordet. | I need help with the word. |
| **veta** | to know | Jag **vet** inte svaret. | I don't know the answer. |
| **höra** | to hear | Jag **hör** läraren. | I hear the teacher. |
| **betyda** | to mean | Vad **betyder** ordet? | What does the word mean? |
| **kunna** | to be able to | Jag **kan** läsa texten. | I can read the text. |

## Classroom words

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en fråga** | a question | Jag har **en fråga**. | I have a question. |
| **ett ord** | a word | Jag skriver **ett ord**. | I write a word. |
| **ett svar** | an answer | Hon ger **ett svar**. | She gives an answer. |
| **en mening** | a sentence | Jag läser **meningen** högt. | I read the sentence aloud. |
| **en text** | a text | Vi läser **texten** tillsammans. | We read the text together. |

## Helpful adverbs

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **inte** | not | Jag hör **inte** ordet. | I don't hear the word. |
| **långsamt** | slowly | Läraren pratar **långsamt**. | The teacher speaks slowly. |
| **igen** | again | Vi läser texten **igen**. | We read the text again. |
| **tydligt** | clearly | Hon säger ordet **tydligt**. | She says the word clearly. |
| **lite** | a little | Jag förstår **lite** svenska. | I understand a little Swedish. |
