| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en månad, månader** | a month, months | Februari är årets kortaste **månad**. | February is the year's shortest month. |
| **januari** | January | Vi åker skidor i **januari**. | We go skiing in January. |
| **februari** | February | Min kurs börjar i **februari**. | My course starts in February. |
| **mars** | March | Våren börjar ofta i **mars**. | Spring often begins in March. |
| **april** | April | Det kan snöa i **april**. | It can snow in April. |
| **maj** | May | Träden blir gröna i **maj**. | The trees turn green in May. |
| **juni** | June | Skolan slutar i **juni**. | School ends in June. |
| **juli** | July | Många har semester i **juli**. | Many people take a holiday in July. |
| **augusti** | August | Vi börjar jobba igen i **augusti**. | We start working again in August. |
| **september** | September | Luften blir kallare i **september**. | The air gets colder in September. |
| **oktober** | October | Löven faller i **oktober**. | The leaves fall in October. |
| **november** | November | Det blir mörkt tidigt i **november**. | It gets dark early in November. |
| **december** | December | Vi firar jul i **december**. | We celebrate Christmas in December. |
