## Talking about languages

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ett språk** | a language | Jag lär mig ett **språk**. | I am learning a language. |
| **ett modersmål** | a native language | Svenska är mitt **modersmål**. | Swedish is my native language. |
| **ett ord** | a word | Jag lär mig ett **ord**. | I am learning a word. |
| **tala** | to speak | Jag **talar** svenska hemma. | I speak Swedish at home. |
| **prata** | to talk, to speak | Vi **pratar** svenska tillsammans. | We speak Swedish together. |
| **förstå** | to understand | Jag **förstår** lite svenska. | I understand a little Swedish. |
| **kunna** | to know, to be able to | Hon **kan** engelska. | She knows English. |
| **läsa** | to read | Jag **läser** på svenska. | I read in Swedish. |
| **skriva** | to write | Han **skriver** på engelska. | He writes in English. |
| **lyssna på** | to listen to | Vi **lyssnar på** svenska ord. | We listen to Swedish words. |
