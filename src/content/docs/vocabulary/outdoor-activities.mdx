## Activities and outings

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en promenad** | a walk | Vi tar **en promenad** i skogen. | We take a walk in the forest. |
| **en utflykt** | an outing | Vi gör **en utflykt** i helgen. | We go on an outing this weekend. |
| **en picknick** | a picnic | Vi har **picknick** i parken. | We have a picnic in the park. |
| **vandring** | hiking | Jag gillar **vandring** i fjällen. | I like hiking in the mountains. |
| **camping** | camping | Vi gillar **camping** på sommaren. | We like camping in summer. |

## Outdoor places and gear

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en park** | a park | Barnen leker i **parken**. | The children play in the park. |
| **en skog** | a forest | Vi promenerar i **skogen**. | We walk in the forest. |
| **en strand** | a beach | Vi badar på **stranden**. | We swim at the beach. |
| **en sjö** | a lake | Hunden simmar i **sjön**. | The dog swims in the lake. |
| **en stig** | a path, a trail | **Stigen** går genom skogen. | The trail goes through the forest. |
| **en cykel** | a bicycle | Jag tar **cykeln** till parken. | I ride my bike to the park. |
| **ett tält** | a tent | Vi sover i **tältet**. | We sleep in the tent. |
| **en ryggsäck** | a backpack | Min **ryggsäck** är tung. | My backpack is heavy. |
| **en grill** | a grill | Vi lagar mat på **grillen**. | We cook food on the grill. |
