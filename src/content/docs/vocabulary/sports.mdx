## Sports

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **fotboll** | football (soccer) | Vi spelar **fotboll** efter skolan. | We play football after school. |
| **ishockey** | ice hockey | Han spelar **ishockey** på vintern. | He plays ice hockey in winter. |
| **tennis** | tennis | Jag spelar **tennis** med min syster. | I play tennis with my sister. |
| **basket** | basketball | De spelar **basket** i skolan. | They play basketball at school. |
| **handboll** | handball | Hon spelar **handboll** varje vecka. | She plays handball every week. |
| **innebandy** | floorball | Vi spelar **innebandy** på fredagar. | We play floorball on Fridays. |
| **simning** | swimming | **Simning** är bra motion. | Swimming is good exercise. |
| **löpning** | running | **Löpning** är bra träning. | Running is good exercise. |
| **skidåkning** | skiing | Vi gillar **skidåkning** på vintern. | We like skiing in winter. |

## Sports words

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en match** | a match | **Matchen** börjar klockan sex. | The match starts at six. |
| **ett lag** | a team | **Vårt lag** spelar i dag. | Our team plays today. |
| **en spelare** | a player | **Spelaren** springer snabbt. | The player runs fast. |
| **en boll** | a ball | **Bollen** ligger på gräset. | The ball is on the grass. |
| **en tränare** | a coach | **Tränaren** hjälper laget. | The coach helps the team. |
| **en idrottshall** | a sports hall | Vi tränar i **idrottshallen**. | We train in the sports hall. |

## Sports actions

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **spela** | to play | Barnen **spelar** fotboll nu. | The children are playing football now. |
| **träna** | to train, to exercise | Jag **tränar** på gymmet. | I work out at the gym. |
| **simma** | to swim | Hon **simmar** i simhallen. | She swims at the swimming pool. |
| **springa** | to run | De **springer** i parken. | They run in the park. |
| **vinna** | to win | Vårt lag **vinner** matchen. | Our team wins the match. |
| **förlora** | to lose | De **förlorar** matchen. | They lose the match. |
| **åka skidor** | to ski | Vi **åker skidor** i fjällen. | We go skiing in the mountains. |
