| **en morfar** | a maternal grandfather | Min **morfar** bor i Göteborg. | My maternal grandfather lives in Gothenburg. |
| **en farmor** | a paternal grandmother | Min **farmor** är snäll. | My paternal grandmother is kind. |
| **en farfar** | a paternal grandfather | Min **farfar** gillar fotboll. | My paternal grandfather likes football. |
| **en moster** | a maternal aunt | Min **moster** har en hund. | My maternal aunt has a dog. |
| **en morbror** | a maternal uncle | Min **morbror** bor i Malmö. | My maternal uncle lives in Malmö. |
| **en faster** | a paternal aunt | Min **faster** arbetar i Stockholm. | My paternal aunt works in Stockholm. |
| **en farbror** | a paternal uncle | Min **farbror** spelar tennis. | My paternal uncle plays tennis. |
| **en kusin** | a cousin | Min **kusin** går i skolan. | My cousin goes to school. |
| **ett barnbarn** | a grandchild | De har **ett barnbarn**. | They have a grandchild. |
| **en släkting** | a relative | Min **släkting** kommer i dag. | My relative is coming today. |
