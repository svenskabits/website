## Counting in Swedish

Here are the Swedish numbers from zero to twenty:

| Number | Swedish |
| ---: | --- |
| 0 | noll |
| 1 | en / ett |
| 2 | två |
| 3 | tre |
| 4 | fyra |
| 5 | fem |
| 6 | sex |
| 7 | sju |
| 8 | åtta |
| 9 | nio |
| 10 | tio |
| 11 | elva |
| 12 | tolv |
| 13 | tretton |
| 14 | fjorton |
| 15 | femton |
| 16 | sexton |
| 17 | sjutton |
| 18 | arton |
| 19 | nitton |
| 20 | tjugo |

The word for **one** changes with the noun: use **en** with common-gender nouns
and **ett** with neuter nouns. For example, **en bok** means “one book,” while
**ett äpple** means “one apple.” When counting on its own, **ett** is commonly
used: **ett, två, tre** (“one, two, three”).

## Tens and larger numbers

Learn these multiples of ten:

| Number | Swedish |
| ---: | --- |
| 30 | trettio |
| 40 | fyrtio |
| 50 | femtio |
| 60 | sextio |
| 70 | sjuttio |
| 80 | åttio |
| 90 | nittio |
| 100 | hundra |

To make most numbers from 21 to 99, put the tens before the ones and write them
as one word: **tjugo + tre = tjugotre** (23), **fyrtio + sju = fyrtiosju**
(47). When the number ends in one and comes before a noun, the final form agrees
with that noun: **tjugoen böcker** (“21 books”), but **tjugoett hus** (“21
houses”).

## Numbers in everyday sentences

Numbers are used much like they are in English for ages, prices, quantities, and
times. Notice that **en** or **ett** for “one” matches the noun it describes:

- **Jag har två biljetter.** — I have two tickets.
- **Hon är trettio år.** — She is thirty years old.
- **Det kostar femtio kronor.** — It costs fifty kronor.
- **Vi behöver fyra stolar.** — We need four chairs.
- **Tåget går om tio minuter.** — The train leaves in ten minutes.

## Useful Expressions

1. **Ett, två, tre!** — One, two, three!
2. **Jag har en bok.** — I have one book.
3. **Jag vill ha ett äpple.** — I want one apple.
4. **Vi är tre personer.** — There are three of us.
5. **Hon har två barn.** — She has two children.
6. **Bussen går om fem minuter.** — The bus leaves in five minutes.
7. **Klockan är sju.** — It is seven o’clock.
8. **Det kostar tjugo kronor.** — It costs twenty kronor.
9. **Han är fyrtio år.** — He is forty years old.
10. **Vi ses klockan åtta.** — See you at eight o’clock.
