## Learning terms

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en kurs** | a course | Jag går på **en kurs** i svenska. | I take a Swedish course. |
| **en lektion** | a lesson | **Lektionen** börjar klockan nio. | The lesson starts at nine. |
| **en lärare** | a teacher | **Läraren** pratar svenska. | The teacher speaks Swedish. |
| **en elev** | a student | **Eleven** läser en text. | The student reads a text. |
| **en bok** | a book | Jag läser **en bok** hemma. | I read a book at home. |
| **en ordbok** | a dictionary | **Ordboken** ligger på bordet. | The dictionary is on the table. |
| **en övning** | an exercise | Vi gör **en övning**. | We do an exercise. |
| **en läxa** | a homework assignment | Jag gör **min läxa** hemma. | I do my homework at home. |
| **ett ord** | a word | Jag lär mig ett nytt **ord**. | I learn a new word. |
| **ett språk** | a language | Jag lär mig **ett språk**. | I am learning a language. |

## Study actions

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **lära sig** | to learn | Jag **lär mig** svenska hemma. | I learn Swedish at home. |
| **studera** | to study | Jag **studerar** svenska på kvällen. | I study Swedish in the evening. |
| **öva** | to practise | Vi **övar** svenska varje dag. | We practise Swedish every day. |
| **läsa** | to read | Jag **läser** en text hemma. | I read a text at home. |
| **skriva** | to write | Hon **skriver** nya ord. | She writes new words. |
| **lyssna på** | to listen to | Jag **lyssnar på** läraren. | I listen to the teacher. |
| **prata** | to speak | Vi **pratar** svenska i skolan. | We speak Swedish at school. |
| **förstå** | to understand | Jag **förstår** texten nu. | I understand the text now. |
| **uttala** | to pronounce | Hon **uttalar** ordet tydligt. | She pronounces the word clearly. |
| **repetera** | to review | Vi **repeterar** orden tillsammans. | We review the words together. |
| **komma ihåg** | to remember | Jag **kommer ihåg** ordet. | I remember the word. |
| **stava** | to spell | Jag **stavar** ordet rätt. | I spell the word correctly. |
