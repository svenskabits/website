| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en fika** | a coffee break with a snack | Vi tar en **fika**. | We are having a coffee break. |
| **fika** | to have coffee and a snack | Vi **fikar** efter jobbet. | We have coffee and a snack after work. |
| **ett café** | a café | Vi ses på ett **café**. | We meet at a café. |
| **en kopp kaffe** | a cup of coffee | Jag tar en **kopp kaffe**. | I'll have a cup of coffee. |
| **en kopp te** | a cup of tea | Hon vill ha en **kopp te**. | She wants a cup of tea. |
| **en kanelbulle** | a cinnamon bun | Jag äter en **kanelbulle**. | I am eating a cinnamon bun. |
| **en bulle** | a bun | Han vill ha en **bulle**. | He wants a bun. |
| **en kaka** | a cookie | Hon äter en **kaka** till kaffet. | She eats a cookie with her coffee. |
| **en smörgås** | a sandwich | Jag tar en **smörgås** också. | I'll have a sandwich too. |
| **bjuda på** | to treat someone to | Jag **bjuder** på kaffe. | I'll treat you to coffee. |
| **träffa** | to meet | Jag **träffar** en vän på caféet. | I meet a friend at the café. |
