## Parts of the day

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en morgon** | a morning | Jag brukar promenera på **morgonen**. | I usually go for a walk in the morning. |
| **en förmiddag** | a late morning | Vi hade ett möte på **förmiddagen**. | We had a meeting in the late morning. |
| **mitt på dagen** | midday | Vi åt lunch **mitt på dagen**. | We ate lunch in the middle of the day. |
| **en eftermiddag** | an afternoon | Jag arbetade hemma i går **eftermiddag**. | I worked from home yesterday afternoon. |
| **en kväll** | an evening | Hon läser ofta på **kvällen**. | She often reads in the evening. |
| **en natt** | a night | Barnet vaknade mitt i **natten**. | The child woke up in the middle of the night. |

## Time expressions

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **i morse** | this morning | Jag missade bussen **i morse**. | I missed the bus this morning. |
| **i förmiddags** | this morning | Jag handlade mat **i förmiddags**. | I did the grocery shopping this morning. |
| **i eftermiddag** | this afternoon | Vi ska träffa våra vänner **i eftermiddag**. | We are meeting our friends this afternoon. |
| **i kväll** | this evening; tonight | Vi lagar middag **i kväll**. | We are making dinner this evening. |
| **i natt** | last night | Jag sov dåligt **i natt**. | I slept badly last night. |
| **i går kväll** | yesterday evening | Vi såg en film **i går kväll**. | We watched a film yesterday evening. |
| **tidigt på morgonen** | early in the morning | Bussen går **tidigt på morgonen**. | The bus leaves early in the morning. |
| **sent på kvällen** | late in the evening | De kom hem **sent på kvällen**. | They came home late in the evening. |
