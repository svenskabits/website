## Celebrations

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en fest** | a party | Vi hade **fest** hemma i lördags. | We had a party at home last Saturday. |
| **ett kalas** | a party, a celebration | Barnen hade **kalas** i trädgården. | The children had a party in the garden. |
| **en födelsedag** | a birthday | Vi firade hennes **födelsedag** med tårta. | We celebrated her birthday with cake. |
| **ett bröllop** | a wedding | De hade ett litet **bröllop** i juni. | They had a small wedding in June. |
| **en studentfest** | a graduation party | Min kusin ordnade **studentfest** efter utspringet. | My cousin had a graduation party after the school-leaving ceremony. |
| **fira** | to celebrate | Vi **firade** examen tillsammans. | We celebrated the graduation together. |
| **en överraskning** | a surprise | Hennes vänner ordnade en **överraskning**. | Her friends arranged a surprise. |

## At the party

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en gäst** | a guest | Alla **gäster** kom före klockan åtta. | All the guests arrived before eight. |
| **en värd** | a host | **Värden** visade oss var maten stod. | The host showed us where the food was. |
| **dekorera** | to decorate | Barnen **dekorerade** bordet med blommor. | The children decorated the table with flowers. |
| **en dekoration** | a decoration | Vi satte upp **dekorationer** i vardagsrummet. | We put up decorations in the living room. |
| **en tårta** | a cake | Hon bakade en **tårta** till festen. | She baked a cake for the party. |
| **ett tilltugg** | a snack | Det fanns **tilltugg** till drycken. | There were snacks with the drinks. |
| **en läsk** | a soft drink | Jag tog en **läsk** till maten. | I had a soft drink with the food. |
| **dansa** | to dance | Vi **dansade** efter middagen. | We danced after dinner. |
| **sjunga** | to sing | Alla **sjöng** med i refrängen. | Everyone sang along to the chorus. |
| **skåla** | to make a toast | De **skålade** för brudparet. | They toasted the couple. |
