| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **aktuell** | current, relevant (not “actual”) | Frågan är fortfarande **aktuell** i debatten. | The issue is still relevant in the debate. |
| **eventuellt** | possibly (not “eventually”) | Vi åker **eventuellt** dit efter mötet. | We might go there after the meeting. |
| **en chef** | a boss, a manager (not a cook) | Min **chef** bad mig leda projektet. | My manager asked me to lead the project. |
| **en semester** | a vacation, a holiday (not a school term) | Vi tar **semester** i juli och reser norrut. | We are taking a vacation in July and travelling north. |
| **ett barn** | a child (not a barn) | Varje **barn** får låna en surfplatta. | Each child can borrow a tablet. |
| **en glass** | an ice cream (not a drinking glass) | Efter maten beställde vi varsin **glass**. | After the meal, we each ordered an ice cream. |
| **rolig** | fun, enjoyable (not “rolling”) | Föreläsningen var både lärorik och **rolig**. | The lecture was both informative and enjoyable. |
| **en fart** | speed (not “fart”) | Bilen höll hög **fart** på motorvägen. | The car was travelling at high speed on the motorway. |
| **ett bad** | a bath (not “bad”) | Efter vandringen tog vi ett varmt **bad**. | After the hike, we had a warm bath. |
| **bra** | good (not a bra) | Hon gjorde ett **bra** intryck på arbetsgivaren. | She made a good impression on the employer. |
| **en by** | a village (not the preposition “by”) | De flyttade till en liten **by** vid kusten. | They moved to a small village by the coast. |
| **ett slut** | an end, an ending (not “slut”) | Romanens **slut** överraskade många läsare. | The novel’s ending surprised many readers. |
| **sluta** | to stop, to quit (not “slut”) | Efter flera år valde hon att **sluta** röka. | After several years, she chose to quit smoking. |
| **kissa** | to pee, to urinate (not “kiss”) | Hunden började gnälla eftersom den behövde **kissa**. | The dog started whining because it needed to pee. |
| **en fabrik** | a factory (not fabric) | **Fabriken** tillverkar reservdelar till tåg. | The factory makes spare parts for trains. |
| **en kostym** | a suit (not a costume) | Han bar en mörk **kostym** på konferensen. | He wore a dark suit to the conference. |
| **sympatisk** | likeable, pleasant (not “sympathetic”) | Hon är **sympatisk** och lätt att samarbeta med. | She is pleasant and easy to work with. |
