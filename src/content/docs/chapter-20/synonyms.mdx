## Adjectives

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **viktig** | important; matters a lot | Det är **viktigt** att alla får komma till tals. | It is important that everyone gets a chance to speak. |
| **betydelsefull** | significant; meaningful | Hennes stöd var **betydelsefullt** för mig. | Her support was meaningful to me. |
| **avgörande** | crucial; decisive | Ett snabbt beslut blev **avgörande** för resultatet. | A quick decision proved crucial to the result. |
| **väsentlig** | essential; fundamental | Det finns en **väsentlig** skillnad mellan förslagen. | There is a fundamental difference between the proposals. |
| **nöjd** | satisfied; content with something | Kunden var **nöjd** med lösningen. | The customer was satisfied with the solution. |
| **belåten** | pleased; content, often with a slightly formal tone | Hon såg **belåten** ut efter mötet. | She looked pleased after the meeting. |
| **lättad** | relieved after worry or pressure | Jag blev **lättad** när provsvaret kom. | I was relieved when the test result arrived. |
| **irriterad** | annoyed; bothered | Han blev **irriterad** över det höga ljudet. | He got annoyed by the loud noise. |
| **frustrerad** | frustrated when something is difficult or blocked | Hon kände sig **frustrerad** över att inget fungerade. | She felt frustrated that nothing was working. |

## Verbs

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **säga** | say; express something in words | Hon **sa** att hon behövde mer tid. | She said that she needed more time. |
| **berätta** | tell; give someone information or a story | Kan du **berätta** vad som hände? | Can you tell me what happened? |
| **meddela** | inform; give someone formal or practical information | Företaget **meddelade** att mötet var inställt. | The company informed us that the meeting was cancelled. |
| **påstå** | claim; state something that may be disputed | Han **påstod** att han inte hade fått mejlet. | He claimed that he had not received the email. |
| **anta** | assume; suppose something is likely | Jag **antar** att tåget blir försenat. | I assume the train will be delayed. |
| **förmoda** | presume; suppose, often with some uncertainty | Vi **förmodar** att rapporten blir klar i dag. | We presume that the report will be ready today. |
| **hjälpa** | help; make a task easier for someone | Min kollega **hjälpte** mig att lösa problemet. | My colleague helped me solve the problem. |
| **stödja** | support; give practical or emotional backing | Föreningen **stöder** familjer i området. | The association supports families in the area. |
| **underlätta** | facilitate; make something easier | Den nya rutinen **underlättar** arbetet. | The new routine makes the work easier. |
| **ändra** | change; make something different | Vi behöver **ändra** tiden för mötet. | We need to change the meeting time. |
| **justera** | adjust; make a small, careful change | Teknikern **justerade** inställningarna. | The technician adjusted the settings. |
| **förändra** | transform; bring about a more substantial change | Digitaliseringen har **förändrat** många yrken. | Digitalisation has transformed many professions. |

## Adverbs

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **kanske** | perhaps; maybe | **Kanske** kan vi hitta en bättre lösning. | Perhaps we can find a better solution. |
| **möjligen** | possibly; perhaps, often more tentative | Hon kommer **möjligen** lite senare. | She may possibly arrive a little later. |
| **sannolikt** | probably; likely | Beslutet fattas **sannolikt** nästa vecka. | The decision will probably be made next week. |
| **därför** | therefore; for that reason | Bussen var inställd, och **därför** tog vi tåget. | The bus was cancelled, so we took the train. |
| **följaktligen** | consequently; as a result, in a formal style | Kostnaderna ökade och **följaktligen** höjdes avgiften. | Costs increased and, consequently, the fee was raised. |
