## Historical periods

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en vikingatid** | a Viking Age | Under **vikingatiden** reste nordbor långt över havet. | During the Viking Age, people from the Nordic region travelled far across the sea. |
| **en medeltid** | a Middle Ages | Många svenska städer växte under **medeltiden**. | Many Swedish towns grew during the Middle Ages. |
| **en stormaktstid** | a great-power period | Sverige var en stormakt under **stormaktstiden** på 1600-talet. | Sweden was a great power during its great-power period in the 17th century. |
| **en industrialisering** | an industrialisation | **Industrialiseringen** förändrade hur många människor arbetade. | Industrialisation changed how many people worked. |

## People and political power

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en viking** | a Viking | En **viking** kunde vara både handelsman och sjöfarare. | A Viking could be both a trader and a seafarer. |
| **en kung** | a king | Gustav Vasa blev **kung** år 1523. | Gustav Vasa became king in 1523. |
| **en drottning** | a queen | Kristina blev **drottning** när hon var barn. | Kristina became queen when she was a child. |
| **ett rike** | a realm | Under medeltiden bestod det svenska **riket** av flera landskap. | During the Middle Ages, the Swedish realm consisted of several provinces. |
| **en union** | a union | Kalmarunionen var en **union** mellan Sverige, Danmark och Norge. | The Kalmar Union was a union between Sweden, Denmark and Norway. |
| **en stormakt** | a great power | Sverige blev en europeisk **stormakt** under 1600-talet. | Sweden became a European great power during the 17th century. |

## Change and society

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en reformation** | a reformation | **Reformationen** förändrade kyrkan i Sverige under 1500-talet. | The Reformation changed the church in Sweden during the 16th century. |
| **ett krig** | a war | **Kriget** påverkade både soldater och deras familjer. | The war affected both soldiers and their families. |
| **fred** | peace | Efter lång tid av strider slöts **fred** mellan länderna. | After a long period of fighting, peace was made between the countries. |
| **en demokrati** | a democracy | I en **demokrati** kan medborgarna påverka politiska beslut. | In a democracy, citizens can influence political decisions. |
| **en rättighet** | a right | Rösträtt är en viktig **rättighet** i en demokrati. | The right to vote is an important right in a democracy. |
| **självständighet** | independence | Landets **självständighet** erkändes av andra stater. | The country's independence was recognised by other states. |
