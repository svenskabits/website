## Migration and residence

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en migration** | migration | **Migration** påverkar både människor och samhällen. | Migration affects both people and societies. |
| **en invandring** | immigration | **Invandringen** har förändrat stadens befolkning över tid. | Immigration has changed the city's population over time. |
| **en utvandring** | emigration | **Utvandringen** från landsbygden ökade under perioden. | Emigration from rural areas increased during the period. |
| **en asyl** | asylum | Hon ansökte om **asyl** efter ankomsten. | She applied for asylum after arriving. |
| **en asylsökande person** | an asylum seeker | **Asylsökande personer** får information om hur processen fungerar. | Asylum seekers receive information about how the process works. |
| **en flykting** | a refugee | **Flyktingen** berättade om sin väg till trygghet. | The refugee spoke about their journey to safety. |
| **ett uppehållstillstånd** | a residence permit | Hon fick **uppehållstillstånd** efter att ansökan hade prövats. | She received a residence permit after her application had been reviewed. |
| **ett medborgarskap** | citizenship | **Medborgarskap** kan ge en starkare känsla av tillhörighet. | Citizenship can give a stronger sense of belonging. |
| **ansöka om** | to apply for | Han ska **ansöka om** uppehållstillstånd. | He is going to apply for a residence permit. |
| **bevilja** | to grant; to approve | Myndigheten **beviljade** hennes ansökan. | The authority approved her application. |
| **avslå** | to reject; to deny | Domstolen **avslog** överklagandet. | The court rejected the appeal. |

## Integration and participation

| Swedish | English | Example (Swedish) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **en integration** | integration | **Integration** är en process som kan se olika ut för olika personer. | Integration is a process that can look different for different people. |
| **en etablering** | establishment; getting established | Språkkunskaper kan underlätta **etableringen** på arbetsmarknaden. | Language skills can make it easier to get established in the labour market. |
| **en inkludering** | inclusion | Skolan arbetar aktivt med **inkludering**. | The school works actively on inclusion. |
| **en delaktighet** | participation; involvement | Föreningen vill öka **delaktigheten** bland sina medlemmar. | The association wants to increase participation among its members. |
| **en arbetsmarknad** | a labour market | Det kan vara svårt att komma in på **arbetsmarknaden** utan kontakter. | It can be hard to enter the labour market without connections. |
| **en språkkunskap** | a language skill | Goda **språkkunskaper** kan öppna fler möjligheter. | Good language skills can open up more opportunities. |
| **delta i** | to take part in | Hon vill **delta i** föreningslivet på sin ort. | She wants to take part in community life where she lives. |
| **bidra till** | to contribute to | Alla kan **bidra till** en tryggare arbetsmiljö. | Everyone can contribute to a safer work environment. |
| **skapa förutsättningar för** | to create the conditions for | Kommunen vill **skapa förutsättningar för** fler möten mellan grannar. | The municipality wants to create the conditions for more encounters between neighbours. |
| **känna sig hemma** | to feel at home | Det tog tid, men nu **känner** hon sig hemma i området. | It took time, but now she feels at home in the neighbourhood. |
